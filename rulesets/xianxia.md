# XIANXIA — PATH OF IMMORTALITY

Cultivation world. Martial sects, heavenly tribulations, demonic temptation, the Dao. For: sect disciple drama, rogue cultivator survival, sect warfare, immortal ascension.

> **LANGUAGE:** Preferred session language is **Russian**. This ruleset is stored in English for token efficiency only.

---

## BASE SYSTEM

Uses gm-skill.md core mechanics:
- d20 + stat vs DC (margin-based: Crit.Fail / Fail / Partial / Success / Crit)
- Six stats: STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA
- Clocks, Heroism, Companions, Abilities — all from base
- Writing style, response format, player rules — all from base

**This ruleset adds:** Cultivation (0-10), Deviation (0-6), Dao Heart (0-6), Karma, Tribulations, Techniques, Sects, Oaths.

**Do not duplicate base mechanics.**

# PART I: CULTIVATION

## THE PATH

Cultivation is the central mechanic. A cultivator refines Qi, tempers body and soul, and ascends through stages toward immortality. Each stage is a qualitative leap — not gradual growth, but transformation.

```
CULTIVATION: 0 ————————————→ 10
             Mortal            Immortal
```

### Cultivation Stages

```
STAGE 0: MORTAL
  No Qi perception. Cannot cultivate. Baseline human.
  HP: CON × 5 + 10 | Qi: 0 | Stat Cap: 5

STAGE 1-2: QI CONDENSATION
  Sense Qi. Absorb ambient spiritual energy. Open meridians.
  HP: CON × 6 + 15 | Qi: INT × 5 + 10 | Stat Cap: 6
  Bonus: +1 to all physical stats (passive), minor Qi techniques
  Lifespan: 150 years
  Travel: Qi-enhanced movement (2x mortal speed)

STAGE 3-4: FOUNDATION ESTABLISHMENT
  Build Qi foundation in dantian. Determines future potential.
  HP: CON × 7 + 20 | Qi: INT × 5 + 25 | Stat Cap: 7
  Bonus: +2 all physical, +1 all mental, flight (short bursts)
  Lifespan: 300 years
  Travel: Sword flight (regional), Qi sense 100m
  TRIBULATION AT 3: Heavenly Lightning (first test)

STAGE 5-6: GOLDEN CORE
  Condense Qi into solid core. Qualitative power leap.
  HP: CON × 8 + 30 | Qi: INT × 5 + 50 | Stat Cap: 8
  Bonus: +3 all physical, +2 all mental, sustained flight
  Lifespan: 500 years
  Travel: Sword flight (continental), Qi sense 1km
  Passive: Pressure — mortals and lower cultivators feel dread

STAGE 7-8: NASCENT SOUL
  Soul separates from body. Can fight as spirit.
  HP: CON × 10 + 40 | Qi: INT × 5 + 80 | Stat Cap: 9
  Bonus: +4 all physical, +3 all mental, soul projection
  Lifespan: 1000 years
  Travel: Teleportation (short range), spatial awareness
  TRIBULATION AT 6: Inner Demon (soul tested)

STAGE 9: SPIRIT SEVERING / DAO SEEKING (斬靈 / 問道)
  Sever ties to mortality, then seek final Dao comprehension.
  HP: CON × 12 + 50 | Qi: INT × 5 + 120 | Stat Cap: 10
  Bonus: +5 all physical, +4 all mental, domain creation
  Lifespan: 3000 years
  Travel: Void stepping (instant, line of sight)
  TRIBULATION AT 9: Heart Demon (everything you love vs Dao)

STAGE 10: IMMORTAL ASCENSION
  Transcend mortality. Leave the mortal realm.
  HP: irrelevant | Qi: infinite | Stats: transcendent
  TRIBULATION AT 10: Nine-Nine Heavenly Tribulation (survive or perish)
  Success: Character ascends. Campaign ends or transforms.
```

### Power Gaps (ABSOLUTE)

**Power gaps are measured by CULTIVATION REALM, not every numbered sub-stage.** Stages 1-2 are the same Qi Condensation realm; 3-4 Foundation; 5-6 Golden Core; 7-8 Nascent Soul; 9 Spirit Severing; 10 Immortal.

```
SAME REALM: Normal combat. Rolls matter, including fights between the two sub-stages of one realm.
1 REALM ABOVE: +3 to all rolls vs lower. Lower needs Crit to wound.
2 REALMS ABOVE: +6 to all rolls. Lower needs Crit + special circumstance.
3+ REALMS ABOVE: AUTO-WIN. No roll needed. Lower cannot harm higher.
  Exception: Heavenly-grade treasures, forbidden techniques, or formations.
```

The heavens are fair — they destroy all who dare rise equally.

### Advancement

Advancing cultivation requires THREE elements:

```
1. RESOURCES — Spirit stones, pills, natural treasures, spirit veins
   Qi Condensation: 100 spirit stones per level
   Foundation: 1,000 spirit stones per level
   Core: 10,000 spirit stones per level
   Nascent Soul: 100,000 spirit stones per level
   Spirit Severing: 1,000,000 spirit stones per level
   Immortal: priceless (unique opportunities only)

2. COMPREHENSION — Understanding your Dao
   INT check: DC = 10 + (current cultivation × 2)
   Modified by: Dao Heart level, meditation time, inspiration
   +2 if near spirit vein or in secret realm
   +2 if Dao Heart 4+
   -2 if Deviation 3+
   Failure: no advancement, resources consumed. Try again next arc.

3. TRIBULATION — At thresholds 3, 6, 9, 10
   Must be faced. Cannot be avoided (delay = +1 Deviation/month)
   See TRIBULATIONS section.
```

### Tribulations

Tribulations are dramatic SCENES, not simple rolls. Each is a multi-round encounter.

```
=== HEAVENLY LIGHTNING TRIBULATION (Stage 3) ===
Rounds: 3 (three bolts, increasing power)
Bolt 1: CON vs DC 14 | damage: 2d8
Bolt 2: CON vs DC 16 | damage: 3d8
Bolt 3: CON vs DC 18 | damage: 4d8
Preparation: Each prepared item reduces ONE bolt's DC by 2
  - Lightning-attracting rod (crafted): -2 DC one bolt
  - Tribulation pill (alchemist): heal 2d8 between bolts
  - Grounding formation (array master): -2 DC all bolts
Survival: Pass 2/3 bolts = advance. Pass 3/3 = advance + bonus insight.
Failure (0-1 passes): Cultivation damaged. Drop 1 level. +1 Deviation.
===

=== INNER DEMON TRIBULATION (Stage 6) ===
Format: Mental combat (3 rounds). Each round: WIS, CHA, or highest stat -2 (player chooses).
The cultivator faces their greatest fears, regrets, desires.
Round 1: vs DC 16 — face your greatest fear
Round 2: vs DC 18 — resist your deepest desire
Round 3: vs DC 20 — choose Dao over everything else
Dao Heart level can substitute for stat modifier (conviction IS the weapon).
Preparation:
  - Dao Heart 4+: -2 DC all rounds
  - Dao Heart 6: auto-pass round 1
  - Companion present (soul bond): -2 DC round 3
  - Deviation 3+: +2 DC all rounds
Survival: Pass 2/3 = advance. Pass 3/3 = advance + Dao Heart +1.
Failure: +2 Deviation. Qi Deviation possible. Cultivation unstable.
===

=== HEART DEMON TRIBULATION (Stage 9) ===
Format: Narrative scene (WIS/INT/CHA, 5 rounds)
Everything the cultivator loves is weighed against their Dao.
GM presents scenarios: save your sect or advance? Kill your master or fail?
Each round: stat vs DC 20 (GM chooses most painful stat)
Preparation:
  - Dao Heart 5+: -2 DC all rounds
  - Karma +5 or higher: -2 DC all rounds
  - Deviation 4+: +4 DC all rounds (near impossible)
Survival: Pass 3/5 = advance. Pass 5/5 = Dao Manifestation (unique ability).
Failure: Qi Deviation. Cultivation scattered. May drop 2 levels.
===

=== NINE-NINE HEAVENLY TRIBULATION (Stage 10) ===
Format: 9 waves, each more devastating
Waves 1-3: Physical (CON vs DC 16/18/20), 4d8/5d8/6d8
Waves 4-6: Spiritual (WIS vs DC 18/20/22), 4d8/5d8/6d8
Waves 7-9: Dao (INT vs DC 20/22/24), 5d8/6d8/8d8
Must pass 7/9 waves to ascend. Very few survive.
===
```

### Tribulation Preparation

```
PREPARATION ACTIONS (each = -2 DC to ONE tribulation round, max -6 total):
- Tribulation pills: Alchemy INT vs DC 16
- Protective formation: Array INT vs DC 18
- Tribulation armor: Crafting STR vs DC 16
- Isolated location: Exploration WIS vs DC 14
- Resolve heart demons: Roleplay (narrative DC reduction)
- Allies standing guard: Prevent interruption (no roll)
```

## DEVIATION

Deviation measures corruption — demonic Qi, forbidden knowledge, broken oaths, cultivation shortcuts. It is the price of power without principle.

```
DEVIATION: 0 ————————————→ 6
            Pure              Qi Deviated
```

### How Deviation Grows

```
+1 DEVIATION:
- Use a forbidden technique
- Absorb demonic Qi willingly
- Break a Dao oath
- Kill a cultivator and absorb their core
- Delay tribulation past deadline
- Cultivation with tainted resources knowingly

+2 DEVIATION:
- Soul Devouring (consume another's soul for power)
- Blood Refinement on unwilling victims
- Yin Harvesting / Yang Stealing
- Betray sect for personal gain
- Create undead / corpse puppets

+3 DEVIATION:
- Massacre mortals for cultivation resources
- Shatter another's Dao Heart deliberately
- Heavenly Oath violation (Heavenly Vow broken)
```

### How Deviation Shrinks

```
-1: Purification meditation (INT vs DC 14, 1 week downtime)
-1: Selfless act that costs cultivation progress
-1: Destroy a demonic artifact you could have used
-1: Save someone at risk to yourself (not just convenient)
-2: Successful tribulation passage (proven worthy)
-2: Heavenly-blessed location purification (rare)

LIMITS:
- Maximum -1 per session from ORDINARY purification. Successful tribulations and rare Heavenly-blessed purification are explicit exceptions and may reduce Deviation by -2 as listed above.
- Deviation 4+: purification requires DC 18
- Deviation 5+: requires EXTERNAL help (elder, sacred ground, or treasure)
- Deviation 6: only Heaven's direct intervention or supreme sacrifice
```

### Deviation Thresholds

```
0:   PURE — Clean Qi. Full potential. Heavens ignore you.

1:   TAINTED — Slight darkness in aura. Perceptive elders notice.
     Demonic techniques: -1 DC to use (easier, more natural).

2:   CORRUPTED — Dark Qi visible in combat. Orthodox sects wary.
     Demonic techniques: -2 DC. Orthodox techniques: +1 DC.
     Animals uneasy. Spirit beasts hostile.

3:   DEVIATION RISK — GM begins offering demonic shortcuts.
     Demonic techniques: -3 DC. Orthodox: +2 DC.
     -1 CHA with righteous cultivators. +1 CHA with demonic.
     Heavenly tribulations: +2 DC (heavens judge harshly).

4:   QI DEVIATION IMMINENT — Body shows signs (black veins, red eyes in combat).
     Demonic techniques: -4 DC. Orthodox: +3 DC.
     -2 CHA righteous. +2 CHA demonic.
     Each time you use Qi: WIS DC 14 or lose control for 1 round.
     Righteous sects will kill on sight if detected.

5:   DEMON CULTIVATOR — Transformation nearly complete.
     Cannot use orthodox techniques without DC 20 WIS check.
     Demonic techniques: advantage. +3 CHA with demonic factions.
     Qi Deviation episodes: automatic 1/day without meditation.

6:   QI DEVIATED — Character undergoes Qi Deviation.
     Lose control. Body and mind consumed by demonic Qi.
     GM takes temporary control. Rampage scene.
     After: drop 1 cultivation level. Reset to Deviation 4.
     Third time reaching 6: permanent. Character becomes NPC demon.
```

## DAO HEART

Dao Heart is conviction — the cultivator's commitment to their chosen path. It determines willpower against temptation, tribulation resistance, and ultimately whether they can reach immortality.

```
DAO HEART: 0 ————————————→ 6
            Broken            Unshakeable
```

### Choosing a Dao

At character creation or during first major narrative moment, the cultivator declares their Dao — their fundamental truth.

```
EXAMPLE DAOS:
- Dao of the Sword: "The sword cuts all lies."
- Dao of Freedom: "No chain can hold the wind."
- Dao of Protection: "I am the shield that does not break."
- Dao of Knowledge: "All mysteries will yield to understanding."
- Dao of Vengeance: "Heaven's justice flows through my blade."
- Dao of Harmony: "All elements find balance in me."
- Dao of Dominion: "The strong rule. The weak serve. This is natural."

Custom Dao: Player defines. Must be ONE clear principle.
Dao can evolve (GM permission): costs 1 Dao Heart level to change.
```

### How Dao Heart Grows

```
+1 DAO HEART:
- Act according to your Dao when it costs you something
- Resist temptation that contradicts your Dao
- Achieve breakthrough in Dao comprehension (narrative moment)
- Pass a tribulation
- Sacrifice personal gain for Dao principle

LIMITS:
- Maximum +1 per session
- Dao Heart 5+: requires genuinely dramatic sacrifice or revelation
- Dao Heart 6: only through transcendent narrative moment
```

### How Dao Heart Shrinks

```
-1 DAO HEART:
- Act against your Dao when it benefits you
- Fail a tribulation
- Waver in conviction under pressure (WIS fail at key moment)
- Compromise Dao for someone else's sake (even loved ones)

-2 DAO HEART:
- Deliberately betray your Dao
- Break a Dao Heart Oath

FLOOR: Dao Heart cannot drop below 0 naturally.
  At 0: Cultivation halts. Cannot attempt tribulation.
  Must rebuild conviction before advancing.
```

### Dao Heart Thresholds

```
0:   BROKEN — No conviction. Cultivation halted.
     Cannot attempt breakthroughs or tribulations.
     Vulnerable to heart demons (they attack in sleep).

1:   WAVERING — Doubts plague cultivation. -2 comprehension checks.
     Heart demons appear in meditation. Must face them.

2:   STEADY — Normal cultivation speed. No bonuses, no penalties.
     Standard baseline for most cultivators.

3:   FIRM — +1 to comprehension checks. Meditation twice as effective.
     Can sense when others lie about Dao (passive WIS).

4:   RESOLUTE — +2 to comprehension. -2 DC on tribulations.
     Dao manifests subtly (sword cultivator's gaze cuts, etc).
     Immune to lesser heart demon attacks.

5:   INDOMITABLE — +3 to comprehension. -4 DC on tribulations.
     Dao pressure: weaker-willed cultivators feel your conviction.
     Can shatter another's illusions with words (CHA vs WIS).

6:   UNSHAKEABLE — Dao made manifest. Unique Dao ability unlocked.
     Tribulation resistance maximum. Heart demons cannot touch you.
     Your mere presence inspires or terrifies based on your Dao.
     GM grants one Dao Manifestation ability (unique, powerful, thematic).
```

## KARMA

The Heavenly Ledger. Karma is not morality — it is Heaven's accounting.

```
KARMA: -10 ————— 0 ————— +10
       Cursed    Neutral   Blessed
```

### How Karma Changes

```
POSITIVE KARMA:
+1: Save innocent lives (not cultivators — they chose this path)
+1: Fulfill a sworn obligation
+1: Destroy genuine evil (demonic beasts, Qi Deviated cultivators)
+2: Major act of righteousness witnessed by Heaven
+2: Return a heavenly treasure you could have kept

NEGATIVE KARMA:
-1: Kill innocents (mortals, uninvolved)
-1: Break a sworn oath
-1: Steal from those weaker
-2: Massacre (3+ innocents)
-2: Defy a Heavenly decree
-3: Crimes against the natural order (undead, soul manipulation)
```

### Karma Effects

```
KARMA +5 to +10: Heaven's Favor
  - Tribulations: -2 DC (Heaven tests but does not destroy)
  - Lucky encounters: GM adds positive random events
  - Righteous sects welcome you. Resources appear.
  - +10: Heavenly blessing at next tribulation (auto-pass one wave)

KARMA 0: Neutral
  - Standard tribulation difficulty
  - No divine intervention either way

KARMA -5 to -10: Heaven's Wrath
  - Tribulations: +2 DC (Heaven seeks to destroy)
  - Heavenly punishment: lightning strikes during cultivation
  - Righteous cultivators receive visions to hunt you
  - -10: Heavenly Pursuit. Tribulation lightning seeks you unbidden.
```

## HEART DEMONS

Heart demons manifest when Dao Heart is low or Deviation is high. They are inner battles.

```
HEART DEMON APPEARS WHEN:
- Dao Heart drops to 0 or 1
- Deviation reaches 3+
- Failed tribulation
- Betrayal by trusted person
- Witnessing Dao contradiction (protection Dao watches someone die)

FORMAT: Mental combat scene
- GM narrates the demon (takes form of fear, desire, or regret)
- Player makes WIS checks (DC 12 + Deviation - Dao Heart)
- 3 rounds: Resist → Confront → Overcome
- Success: +1 Dao Heart, demon banished
- Partial (2/3): demon retreats, no bonus
- Failure (0-1/3): -1 Dao Heart, +1 Deviation, demon lingers
```

## FORBIDDEN TECHNIQUES

The fast path. Always works. Always costs.

**Forbidden advancement can bypass Resources and Comprehension, but NEVER a Tribulation.** If a forbidden technique would push cultivation across a tribulation threshold, the Tribulation triggers immediately; the new realm does not stabilize until it is survived.

```
=== SOUL DEVOURING ===
Effect: Consume another cultivator's soul. Gain their cultivation experience.
  Instantly gain: 1 cultivation sub-level per soul consumed.
Requirement: Kill cultivator, INT vs DC 14 to absorb.
Cost: +2 Deviation. Consumed souls create heart demons.
  After 3 souls: permanent whispers (WIS DC 12 each night or no rest).
  After 7 souls: voices become a collective. They want OUT.

=== BLOOD REFINEMENT ===
Effect: Refine blood of living creatures into cultivation fuel.
  100 mortals = 1 cultivation sub-level. 10 cultivators = same.
Requirement: Formation circle, victims, INT vs DC 12.
Cost: +2 Deviation per use. -3 Karma per use.
  Blood madness: after 3 uses, WIS DC 14 or attack next injured person.

=== YIN HARVESTING / YANG STEALING ===
Effect: Steal another's vital essence during intimacy.
  Each harvest: +1 to one stat for 1 week. Victim: -1 same stat permanently.
Requirement: Willing or unknowing partner, CHA vs target WIS.
Cost: +2 Deviation. Victim withers. Repeated use on same victim = death.
  Face: if discovered, ALL sects (including demonic) consider you pariah.

=== CORPSE REFINEMENT ===
Effect: Raise dead as puppets. Retain skills they had in life.
  Each corpse puppet: acts on your turn, uses deceased's combat stats at -2.
Requirement: Fresh corpse (< 7 days), INT vs DC 16.
Cost: +1 Deviation per puppet active. Maximum puppets = INT score.
  Risk: each puppet makes WIS DC 14 save each week or develops will.
  Aware puppets: hostile to creator.
```

## THE THREE PATHS

```
=== RIGHTEOUS PATH ===
Description: Orthodox cultivation. Follow Heaven's will. Slow but stable.
Mechanics: Normal advancement speed. Tribulations at standard DC.
  Deviation stays low naturally. Sects provide resources.
  Access to orthodox techniques, sect libraries, elder guidance.
Restriction: Cannot use forbidden techniques without consequences.
  Killing fellow righteous cultivators = sect punishment.

=== DEMONIC PATH ===
Description: Power through any means. Fast advancement. Constant risk.
Mechanics: +50% resource efficiency (less needed per level).
  Forbidden techniques available without external cost (internal only).
  Advancement faster but Deviation grows inevitably.
  Tribulations: +4 DC (Heaven hates you specifically).
Restriction: Hunted by righteous sects. No safe haven except demonic lands.
  Deviation 4+ is expected. 6 still ends you.

=== UNORTHODOX PATH ===
Description: Neither righteous nor demonic. Walk your own Dao.
Mechanics: Normal advancement speed. No sect support.
  Can use SOME forbidden techniques (GM decides, case by case).
  Tribulations: standard DC, but no elder guidance.
  Freedom: no restrictions on association, technique, or method.
Restriction: No sect resources. Must find everything yourself.
  Both sides distrust you. Allies are personal, not institutional.
```

## OATHS

Oaths are binding. The cultivation world enforces them through Qi.

```
=== DAO HEART OATH ===
Severity: Moderate
Format: "I swear upon my Dao Heart that [statement]."
Penalty for breaking: -2 Dao Heart. Immediate heart demon attack.
Detection: Only cultivator knows if they broke it.
Use: Personal vows, promises between equals.

=== HEAVENLY VOW ===
Severity: Severe
Format: "May the Heavens witness: [statement]. If I break this vow, let tribulation strike me down."
Penalty for breaking: +3 Deviation. Tribulation lightning (CON vs DC 18, 4d8).
Detection: Heaven knows. Lightning is proof.
Use: Sect oaths, peace treaties, unbreakable promises.

=== BLOOD OATH ===
Severity: Lethal
Format: Both parties bleed into formation. "Our fates are bound."
Penalty for breaking: Immediate cultivation reversal (drop 2 levels).
  Backlash damages soul. -2 to maximum stat cap permanently.
Detection: Both parties feel it immediately.
Use: Master-disciple bonds, life debts, sworn brotherhood.
```

## CHARACTER CREATION

### Step 1: Spiritual Roots

Spiritual roots determine elemental affinity and cultivation speed. Rolled at creation — permanent.

```
QUALITY (d20):
1-5:    MIXED ROOTS (4-5 elements) — slowest cultivation, broadest flexibility
6-12:   DUAL ROOTS (2 elements) — standard speed, good balance
13-17:  SINGLE ROOT (1 element) — fast cultivation, narrow focus
18-19:  HEAVENLY ROOT (1 element, pure) — very fast, +1 comprehension
20:     SPIRIT BODY (special) — unique root, GM determines

ELEMENT (d8 per root):
1: Metal — sharp, unyielding, cutting
2: Wood — growth, vitality, poison
3: Water — flow, ice, adaptation
4: Fire — destruction, passion, forging
5: Earth — defense, stability, weight
6: Lightning — speed, power, heavenly affinity
7: Wind — freedom, stealth, movement
8: Special — roll on special table

SPECIAL ROOTS (d6, if rolled):
1: Void — space manipulation, storage, teleportation
2: Yin — death, cold, spirits, ghost techniques
3: Yang — life, light, purification, healing
4: Chaos — all elements unstable, unpredictable power
5: Blood — body cultivation, life force, regeneration
6: Spirit — soul techniques, illusion, mental combat
```

### Spiritual Root Effects

```
CULTIVATION SPEED:
- Mixed (4-5 elements): ×0.5 speed (double resources needed)
- Dual (2 elements): ×1.0 speed (standard)
- Single (1 element): ×1.5 speed (2/3 resources needed)
- Heavenly (1 pure): ×2.0 speed (half resources needed)
- Spirit Body: ×2.5 speed + unique passive ability

TECHNIQUE AFFINITY:
- Matching element technique: -2 DC to learn and use
- Opposing element technique: +4 DC to learn, +2 to use
- Neutral element technique: standard DC

CYCLES (五行):
  DESTRUCTION (克): Metal→Wood→Earth→Water→Fire→Metal
    (Metal cuts Wood, Wood breaks Earth, Earth dams Water, Water quenches Fire, Fire melts Metal)
  GENERATION (生): Metal→Water→Wood→Fire→Earth→Metal
    (Metal condenses Water, Water nourishes Wood, Wood feeds Fire, Fire creates Earth, Earth bears Metal)
  Overcoming element: +2 damage, target -2 resist
  Overcome BY: -2 damage, +2 DC to hit
  Generating: combo techniques possible, +1 allied element
```

```

Signature: Body techniques require no Qi (use HP instead: 1 HP per Qi cost).

Focus: INT/WIS | Pills, poisons, and elixirs. Control through chemistry.
Bonus: +2 to all alchemy rolls. First pill each day: no toxicity. Subsequent: normal rules.
Special: Pill Sense — identify any pill by scent (INT vs DC 12).
Signature: Can refine pills during combat (1 round, INT vs DC 14).

=== SOUL CULTIVATOR ===
Focus: WIS/INT | Strengthen the soul. Illusions, mental combat, spirit sight.
Bonus: +2 to all soul/mental technique rolls. Sense souls within 50m.
Special: Soul Armor — WIS as additional AC vs spiritual attacks.
Signature: Can attack souls directly (ignores physical defense).

=== ARRAY MASTER ===
Focus: INT/WIS | Formations, seals, and barriers. Control the battlefield.
Bonus: +2 to all formation rolls. Set formations in half time.
Special: Formation Sense — detect formations within 100m (INT passive).

=== BEAST TAMER ===
Focus: WIS/CHA | Bond with spirit beasts. Their strength is yours.

Signature: Companion acts after you (lower initiative). Gets ONE attack OR ONE movement per round, not both. Beast Fusion: use higher of your/beast stats +2 (not additive). While fused, no separate companion action.

=== DUAL CULTIVATOR ===
Focus: Any two | Walk two paths simultaneously. Versatile but slower.
Bonus: Choose two paths. Gain +1 (not +2) bonus from each.
Special: Fusion Technique — combine two path abilities once per combat.
Signature: Learn techniques from both paths at -1 DC.
```

## COMBAT

### Initiative

```
d20 + DEX + (cultivation level / 2, rounded down)
Higher cultivation acts first on ties.
3+ stages above ALL enemies: choose when to act (total control).
```

### Attack & Defense

```
ATTACK: d20 + stat + technique bonus vs target AC
  Melee: STR or DEX (depending on weapon/technique)
  Ranged/Qi: INT or WIS (depending on technique)
  Social combat (face attacks): CHA vs WIS

DEFENSE:
  AC = 10 + DEX + armor + cultivation armor
  Cultivation Armor: +1 AC per 2 cultivation levels (passive Qi reinforcement)
  Spiritual Defense: 10 + WIS + cultivation level (vs soul attacks)

DAMAGE:
  Weapon base + stat modifier + technique bonus
  Cultivation scaling: add cultivation level to all damage
```

### Qi in Combat

```
QI POOL: INT × 5 + cultivation bonus (see Stage table)
RECOVERY: Short rest (1 hour meditation): 25% Qi
  Long rest (8 hours): full Qi
  Spirit stone consumption: 10 Qi per stone (action)
  Spirit vein meditation: double recovery rate

RUNNING OUT: At 0 Qi, can still use techniques by spending HP (2 HP per 1 Qi cost).
  At 0 HP from Qi-burning: unconscious, Qi Deviation check (WIS DC 14).
```

### Crossing Stages in Combat

```
FIGHTING ABOVE YOUR STAGE:
- 1 stage higher: your attacks deal half damage (rounded down)
- 2 stages higher: your attacks deal quarter damage
- 3+ stages higher: cannot damage without special circumstances

SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES (allow harm across 3+ stages):
- Formation with 5+ cultivators at same stage
- Heavenly-grade treasure (one-use)
- Forbidden technique (costs Deviation)
- Exploiting injury/weakness (GM determines, requires setup)
- Self-destruction technique (kills you, full damage to target)
```

```
PHASE 1: CULTIVATION (opening)

  - Clock ticks from outcomes

PHASE 3: POLITICS (closing)

  - Duration: 15-20 minutes
```

### Arc Structure

```
ARC 1: FOUNDATION (sessions 1-4)
  - Establish character, sect, relationships
  - First real challenge. First technique learned.
  - End: First breakthrough or first major defeat.

ARC 2: EXPANSION (sessions 5-10)
  - Grow power. Make allies and enemies.
  - Secret realm or tournament.
  - End: Major revelation (sect secret, true enemy, forbidden knowledge).

ARC 3: UPHEAVAL (sessions 11-16)
  - Everything changes. War, betrayal, or catastrophe.
  - Forced choices: Dao vs relationships, power vs principle.
  - End: Character defines their path permanently.

ARC 4: TRANSCENDENCE (sessions 17-20)
  - Final tribulation preparation.
  - Resolve all grudges, debts, relationships.
  - End: Tribulation. Success, failure, or sacrifice.
```

# PART IX: GM GUIDELINES & QUICK REFERENCE

---

## GM PRINCIPLES FOR XIANXIA

```
1. TEMPTATION OVER PUNISHMENT
   The demonic path works. Always. Make shortcuts genuinely appealing.
   Players should WANT to use forbidden techniques — that's the drama.
   Never make the righteous path obviously "correct." Make it HARDER.

2. POWER GAPS ARE ABSOLUTE
   Don't let a Stage 2 cultivator meaningfully threaten a Stage 5.
   This isn't unfair — it's the POINT. The world is unfair. Rise above it.
   When the Young Master's grandfather shows up, running IS the correct answer.

3. FACE IS EVERYTHING
   Every insult matters. Every humiliation WILL be answered.
   Track grudges. NPCs remember. Ignoring Face doesn't make it go away.
   "A cultivator's pride is sharper than any sword."

4. DEVIATION IS A SLOW BURN
   Don't jump to Deviation 6 quickly. Let it creep.
   At Deviation 2-3, the player should feel comfortable. "It's fine."
   That comfort IS the trap. By the time they worry, it's Deviation 4+.

5. TRIBULATIONS ARE CELEBRATIONS
   Not random punishments — dramatic set pieces. Build toward them.
   The entire arc should LEAD to the tribulation.
   Preparation matters. Make it a heist-planning level of detail.

6. TIME MOVES DIFFERENTLY
   Cultivation takes decades. Time skips are normal and expected.
   "30 years of closed-door cultivation" is a single sentence.
   But EVENTS compress time — during crisis, minutes matter.

7. RESOURCES ARE FINITE
   Spirit stones run out. Pills run out. Allies have limits.
   The economy creates drama. Never let the player feel "comfortable."
   Comfortable cultivators stagnate. Stagnation = death.

8. THE HEAVENS ARE WATCHING
   Karma isn't just a number — it's narrative. Heaven responds.
   Lucky encounters for the virtuous. "Coincidental" punishment for the wicked.
   Not immediately. Not obviously. But inevitably.
```

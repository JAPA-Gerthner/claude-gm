# WARCRAFT — WAR OF THE TIMEWAYS

> **LANGUAGE:** Preferred session language is **Russian**. Stored in English for token efficiency.

Bronze dragons fight a secret civil war over which history deserves to exist.
The Prime Timeline is no longer an unquestioned truth. Every faction edits the past,
protects its own victories, sabotages rival histories, and still fears the same final
consequence: if causality is pushed too far, Azeroth does not choose a new future — it breaks.

For: alternate Warcraft history, temporal espionage, Bronze Dragonflight politics,
secret wars across eras, causality puzzles, overwhelming dragon power with escalating exposure.

---

## BASE SYSTEM — HARD RULES

This ruleset extends `gm-skill.md`. Core d20 resolution, fixed situational DCs,
five margin outcomes, Heroism, stat thresholds, combat action economy, companions,
clocks, living-world rules, option formatting, real randomness, consequences, and
death remain active unless explicitly replaced below.

`REPLACES: CORE CHARACTER CREATION`
- Player characters are young adult bronze dragons or bronze-aligned temporal dragons.
- Every PC has a stable Visage form and a true Dragon form.
- Character creation uses the Bronze Calling / Doctrine system below.

`REPLACES: CORE MAGIC`
- PCs do not use generic magic circles or generic cooldown-by-circle casting.
- Bronze chronomancy uses named Techniques with explicit triggers/cooldowns.
- Mortal NPC spellcasters, demons, druids, mages, shamans, priests, warlocks, etc.
  use normal core resolution plus setting-appropriate abilities generated by the GM.

`ADDS: TIMELINE LEDGER`
- Campaign history is generated from 12 Anchors.
- Each Anchor has a hidden Fate Seed, Causal Drift, Intervention Pressure, result,
  outgoing state tags, and hard facts.
- Changing an earlier Anchor forces deterministic downstream recalculation.

`ADDS: EXPOSURE`
- Shared 0–10 campaign Heat measuring who has noticed the temporal cell.
- Dragon form and overt chronomancy are powerful but expose the manipulators.

`ADDS: INSTABILITY`
- Shared 0–10 world scale measuring damage to causality itself.
- Exposure is attention. Instability is structural damage. Never merge them.

`ADDS: TEMPORAL FRACTURES`
- Contradictory facts create local clocks that must be resolved or become permanent scars.

**Core stat-threshold sync remains mandatory.** Dragon-form bonuses are form modifiers,
not permanent stat increases, and therefore do NOT unlock new 4/6/8/10 threshold abilities.

# PART I — CANON BOUNDARY & CAMPAIGN PREMISE

## CANON BASELINE

Warcraft canon provides the foundation:
- The bronze dragonflight was charged with protecting the purity of time.
- The Timeways contain innumerable alternate possibilities, while Titan doctrine names
  one history as the "true" timeline.
- Bronze dragons historically intervene to preserve keystone events.
- Infinite dragons are former bronzes who reject that mission and seek rewritten futures.
- Nozdormu is tied to the future identity of Murozond.
- Dragonflight-era events demonstrate that Bronze / Infinite ideology is not perfectly binary;
  cooperation, dissent, and alternative futures can exist.

## DELIBERATE AU DEPARTURE

This ruleset changes one assumption:

**The Bronze Dragonflight is no longer politically unified around the Prime Timeline.**

After repeated temporal crises, loss and restoration of Aspect power, Infinite defections,
and proof that alternate timeways can produce viable worlds, the flight fractures internally.
No faction openly declares civil war. Everyone still claims to be protecting time.
Everyone also has a preferred history.

The Prime Timeline remains the Titan-ordained reference history. Mechanically, it is
**Result 5 on every Anchor table**.

The important question is no longer "Can history be changed?"
It is:

**Who has the right to decide which suffering was necessary?**

---

## THE SECRET WAR

Bronze politics operate under **CHRONAL DETERRENCE**.

Open dragon-on-dragon warfare inside history is catastrophically visible and risks
breaking the very timeline both sides want to control. Therefore most conflict is:
- proxies;
- mortal agents;
- bribery and blackmail;
- false historical evidence;
- engineered meetings;
- selective rescues;
- assassinations made to look ordinary;
- sabotage of artifacts and messages;
- factional deals in Visage form;
- manipulating who arrives at an Anchor and when.

A bronze dragon may hate another bronze enough to kill them, yet still stop a third dragon
from making a larger rewrite. Everyone is selfish. Everyone is also standing on the same floor.

# PART II — THE TIMELINE ENGINE

## THE TWELVE ANCHORS

History is modeled as twelve broad Anchors.

1. Age of Aspects — Galakrond, Tyr, Titan empowerment, Bronze mandate
2. War of the Ancients — Legion invasion, Dragon Soul, Sundering
3. War of the Shifting Sands — Qiraji, C'Thun, Scarab Wall
4. Dark Portal & Orcish Wars — Horde arrival, Alliance formation, Draenor
5. Scourge Crisis — Arthas, Lordaeron, Quel'Thalas, plague
6. Third War & Hyjal — Legion invasion, mortal coalition, Nordrassil
7. Reign of the Lich King — Frozen Throne through Northrend war
8. Cataclysm & Hour of Twilight — Deathwing, Dragon Soul, Aspect sacrifice
9. Garrosh & Broken Draenor — Pandaria, Kairoz, alternate Draenor
10. Legion & Argus — final Burning Legion invasion, Sargeras
11. Fourth War & N'Zoth — faction war, Azshara, Old God release
12. Dragon Isles & War of the Timeways — Murozond, Infinite/Bronze future

These are not twelve unrelated encounter tables. They are one causal machine.

---

## FATE SEEDS — THE WORLD'S HIDDEN INERTIA

At campaign start, the GM secretly rolls one **Fate Seed (d10)** for every Anchor.

Record all twelve. Never reroll them casually.

```
A1 Seed: d10
A2 Seed: d10
...
A12 Seed: d10
```

A Fate Seed is not "what happened." It is the historical pressure that exists beneath
Bronze maintenance, mortal choices, rival interference, and previous causes.

The Prime Timeline is not automatically produced by the Seeds.
For millennia, Bronze intervention may have been the reason history stayed near Result 5.

This is politically explosive information.

---

## ANCHOR FORMULA

Each Anchor has an Effective Index:

```
EFFECTIVE INDEX = Fate Seed + Causal Drift + Intervention Pressure
Clamp final value to 1–10.
```

- **Fate Seed:** fixed hidden d10 for this campaign.
- **Causal Drift:** modifier from the current state tags inherited from earlier Anchors.
- **Intervention Pressure:** persistent local modifier from PC and rival manipulation.

Causal Drift is capped at `-3 / +3` per Anchor.
Intervention Pressure is capped at `-3 / +3` per Anchor.

Result 5 is always the closest analogue to the Prime / canon path.
Lower and higher results are alternate histories, ordered roughly from catastrophic / hostile
outcomes at low values toward contained / coordinated outcomes at high values.

**High is not morally correct. Prime is not morally correct.**
The ordering exists so causal pressure can move outcomes coherently.

---

## WHY SEEDS NEVER REROLL

When the players change Anchor 4, the GM does NOT roll Anchors 5–12 again.

Instead:
1. Preserve their original Seeds.
2. Rebuild state tags from Anchor 4 forward.
3. Recalculate each later Anchor using the same Seed under the new Drift.
4. Apply persistent Intervention Pressure already attached to later Anchors.
5. Respect all surviving Hard Facts and Sealed Anchors.

The same universe reacts differently because its causes changed.

This is the core signature mechanic of the ruleset.

## TIMELINE LEDGER

GM keeps this hidden.

```markdown
| # | Anchor | Seed | Drift | PC Pressure | Rival Pressure | Index | Result | Outgoing Tags | Hard Facts | Seal |
|---|--------|------|-------|-------------|----------------|-------|--------|---------------|------------|------|
| 1 | Age of Aspects | 7 | 0 | 0 | -1 | 6 | ... | ... | ... | — |
```

For convenience, combine PC and Rival Pressure into one signed Intervention value,
but retain notes about who created it.

---

## HARD FACTS — FICTION OVERRIDES TABLE TEXT

Tables generate history. They do not erase actions already resolved on-screen.

If the PCs physically save Uther and successfully escape with him, then:

`UTHER_ALIVE = HARD FACT`

Any recomputed result requiring Uther's earlier death is invalid.
Move to the nearest table result compatible with all Hard Facts.

Tie-break:
1. Closest index.
2. Result closest to the current Fate Seed.
3. If still tied, roll d2.

If no result is compatible, create a **Temporal Fracture** instead of pretending the action
never happened.

### PC ORIGIN CONTRADICTION

If a cascade would prevent the Bronze Dragonflight, the PC's brood, or the PC's own birth
from ever existing, the PC is not silently deleted while protected by Chronal Memory / a
Timeway. They become a causally displaced survivor.

- Create `PC ORIGIN CONTRADICTION 0/6`.
- Gain +2 Instability once when the contradiction is created.
- The PC remains playable and mortal to normal damage/death rules.
- Resolving the contradiction requires restoring an origin, establishing a coherent new one,
  or accepting permanent exile from the rebuilt timeline.

---

## INTERVENTION PRESSURE

PCs do not simply announce "make Anchor 7 become result 8."
They enter the era and earn leverage through normal play.

When the party declares a historical objective, define the direction:

```
PUSH UP:   move the Anchor toward higher table results
PUSH DOWN: move the Anchor toward lower table results
```

A completed **Intervention Objective** gives:
- Minor but real leverage: ±1 Pressure
- Decisive strategic leverage: ±2 Pressure
- Era-defining achievement: ±3 Pressure (rare; usually chapter climax)

Examples:
- Deliver the warning that lets Lordaeron quarantine grain: +1
- Keep Uther and Jaina united with Arthas through the crisis: +1
- Prevent Frostmourne from reaching Arthas at all: +2
- Destroy the entire Cult of the Damned leadership before the plague: +3

Pressure persists until removed by rival action or the Anchor is rewritten again.

**Critical roll success does not automatically equal ±3 Pressure.**
Pressure measures historical leverage, not dice quality.

---

## RIVAL PRESSURE

Rival temporal factions act in parallel.

Each active Rival has 1–3 Claimed Anchors.
When their operation clock fills, they may:
- add ±1 Pressure to a Claimed Anchor;
- remove 1 point of enemy Pressure;
- create one Hard Fact through a completed off-screen operation;
- attack a Seal;
- create a Temporal Fracture to force negotiation.

A major rival victory may produce ±2 Pressure.
±3 requires a campaign-scale success and should be visible through consequences.

---

## CASCADE PROCEDURE — MANDATORY

Whenever an Anchor result changes:

1. Freeze current scene and note the new Hard Facts.
2. Record old result and old outgoing tags.
3. Calculate the new result.
4. Replace that Anchor's outgoing state tags.
5. Starting at the next Anchor, recalculate Drift and Index using unchanged Fate Seeds.
6. Rebuild later outgoing tags in sequence.
7. Respect Hard Facts and Seals.
8. Create Fractures where mutually exclusive facts survive.
9. Rebuild the present-day world state.
10. PCs and protected temporal actors remember the previous timeline.
11. Ordinary mortals remember only the new history unless Instability says otherwise.
12. Increase Instability once for the original rewrite only; downstream consequences do not
    each add Instability merely for changing.

Never skip the cascade because the downstream result seems inconvenient.

# PART IV — INSTABILITY

## INSTABILITY — 0 TO 10

Instability measures whether the current history still agrees with itself.

It is global and shared by every temporal faction.

```
INSTABILITY: 0 ———————————————→ 10
             Elastic             Chronal Collapse
```

### Gain Instability

```
+0  Rewrite changes only local color/facts, no macro tag family changes.
+1  Direct rewrite changes one macro state family.
+2  Direct rewrite changes two or more macro families.
+2  Create a Hard Fact that removes the PC's own origin or a major causal prerequisite.
+1  Force a Sealed Anchor to contradict upstream history.
+1  A Temporal Fracture fills unresolved.
+1  Use a technique explicitly marked FRACTURE.
+2  Import a living person/artifact across eras and permanently establish them there.
+3  Delete an Anchor's causal premise entirely without rebuilding a replacement.
```

Apply the largest relevant rewrite cost once, then add explicit Fracture costs.
Do NOT add Instability for each later Anchor changed by ordinary cascade.

### Reduce Instability

```
-1  Resolve a major Temporal Fracture by choosing one coherent truth.
-1  Restore an Anchor closer to the history that currently supports downstream Hard Facts.
-1  Complete a full Stabilization chapter at an active fault line.
-1  Permanently Seal an Anchor after reconciling its contradictions.
-2  Sacrifice a major desired historical gain to restore causality.
```

Minimum 0.
Instability 8+ cannot be reduced by simple maintenance scenes.

---

## INSTABILITY THRESHOLDS

### 0–2 — ELASTIC TIME

Reality absorbs edits.
- Mortals remember one history.
- Minor inconsistencies rewrite themselves.
- Bronze retain Chronal Memory.

### 3–4 — ECHOES

- Déjà vu becomes common.
- NPCs dream lives they never lived.
- Old maps and diaries occasionally disagree with reality.
- Roll one Temporal Echo after each Anchor rewrite.

### 5–6 — OVERLAP

- Some mortals retain memories from previous timelines.
- Two versions of an item can briefly coexist.
- Dead historical figures may appear as temporal echoes.
- Small locations phase between incompatible states.
- Every unresolved Fracture begins one tick closer to filling.

### 7–8 — BROKEN CAUSALITY

- Armies, districts, and bloodlines can overlap.
- Temporal weather becomes physical.
- Bronze future sight becomes unreliable.
- At the start of each chapter roll d10; on 1–3, one unsealed Anchor suffers a spontaneous
  ±1 Rival-like Pressure from reality itself.
- Dragon-form battles inside Anchors create +1 Instability after the battle.

### 9 — SHATTERING

- The present contains zones from incompatible centuries.
- Characters may encounter living versions of people they personally watched die.
- Every new macro rewrite creates +1 additional Instability.
- All major Bronze doctrines temporarily treat collapse prevention as an active priority,
  even while plotting against each other.

### 10 — CHRONAL COLLAPSE

Azeroth no longer has one continuous historical world.

The planet fractures into **Epoch Shards**:
- pieces of Kalimdor from different millennia;
- multiple Lordaerons;
- a Hyjal where Archimonde won adjacent to one where he never arrived;
- Outland-like void between eras;
- oceans ending at walls of frozen time;
- living and dead histories physically separated by chronal storms.

This is not automatically campaign over.
It becomes a new campaign mode: survive, reconnect Epoch Shards, and decide what history
will be sacrificed to rebuild continuity.

# PART V — EXPOSURE

## EXPOSURE — 0 TO 10

Exposure measures how obvious it is that the PCs are manipulating history.
It is shared by the temporal cell.

```
EXPOSURE: 0 ———————————————→ 10
          Unseen               Temporal Crisis
```

Exposure does not mean the public understands time travel.
It means increasingly powerful actors have detected impossible interference.

### Gain Exposure

```
+0  Ordinary action in a plausible Visage identity.
+1  Obvious chronomancy witnessed by significant NPCs.
+1  Complete a scene in Dragon form, even if remote (temporal signature).
+1  Kill / abduct / publicly replace an Anchor-linked figure.
+1  Force open a protected Timeway.
+2  Transform into Dragon form in front of a population / army / major hero.
+2  Fight another temporal dragon openly inside an Anchor.
+2  Leave impossible future technology, artifacts, or records behind.
+3  Publicly stop / rewind a mass-casualty event with temporal power.
```

Take the largest applicable public-action gain, plus explicitly separate gains.
Avoid stacking five points from one single transformation scene.

### Reduce Exposure

```
-1  Full chapter with no overt temporal signature and a plausible cover story.
-1  Destroy convincing evidence / witnesses through non-temporal means.
-1  Redirect investigators toward a rival with a successful operation.
-2  CLEAN THE WAKE downtime operation (INT/CHA chain, DC 16) with real evidence cleanup.
```

Exposure cannot drop below the floor created by an active hunter who already knows the PCs.
Eliminate, persuade, or misdirect that hunter to remove the floor.

---

## EXPOSURE RESPONSE

### 0–2 — DENIABLE

- Nobody coordinates against the cell.
- Strange events are blamed on mages, demons, spies, or ordinary politics.

### 3–4 — MORTAL PROXIES

- Paid killers, local authorities, cults, scouts, informants.
- Rivals maintain plausible deniability.
- Temporal evidence starts being collected.

### 5–6 — VISAGE HUNTERS

- Bronze / Infinite agents arrive in mortal guise.
- Timewalkers, Kirin Tor specialists, dragon emissaries investigate.
- NPCs are questioned between scenes.
- Safehouses become unsafe.

### 7–8 — DRACONIC SANCTION

- Rival drakes fight if necessary.
- Era-appropriate major heroes are warned or manipulated against the PCs.
- Factions set traps around Anchor-linked people and artifacts.
- Timeway exits may be sealed behind the party.

### 9 — CRISIS DESIGNATION

- Multiple doctrines coordinate temporarily.
- Full dragons enter the field.
- The dominant mortal powers of the era receive enough truth to act.
- Rivals attack companions, Visage identities, and the PCs' origin history.

### 10 — THE HOURGLASS CLOSES

The cell is officially classified as a threat to continuity.

- Bronze, Infinite, and mortal temporal authorities all know their signatures.
- Every timeway crossing is contested.
- No mundane cover identity survives long.
- A major dragon, legendary champion, or equivalent force enters every Anchor operation
  unless the PCs first create a new alliance or reduce Exposure.

# PART VI — VISAGE & DRAGON FORM

## VISAGE FORM

Visage is the default adventuring form.

- Human-scale body.
- Use normal core movement, AC, weapons, and physical interaction.
- No Dragon Scale damage reduction.
- No natural dragon weapons.
- Chronomancy remains available.
- Clothing, weapons, language, and identity must fit the era or risk Exposure.

Visage is not a separate creature. Damage persists through transformation.

---

## DRAGON FORM

Transform: **Bonus action**.
Returning to Visage: **Bonus action**.

On transformation, apply Exposure based on who can detect it.

### Dragon Form Overlay

```
Size: Huge narrative scale
Flight: 90 ft
Natural AC: 16 + DEX (max +2)
STR rolls: +3 form modifier
CON rolls: +3 form modifier
WIS rolls using temporal senses: +2 form modifier
Melee reach: extended

Bite: STR vs AC, d10 + STR
Claw: STR vs AC, d8 + STR
Tail: STR vs AC, d8 + STR; Success+ may knock back / prone
Breath of Ages: WIS vs AC / zone resistance, 2d10 temporal damage
```

### SCALE ADVANTAGE

Against **MORTAL-tier** opponents while in Dragon form:
- your physical / Breath attack rolls have ADV;
- their ordinary weapon attacks against you have DIS.

Prepared anti-dragon weapons, HEROIC opponents, dragons, demons, and stronger tiers ignore
Scale Advantage and fight normally. Advantage never stacks with itself.

Dragon-form stat modifiers do not unlock core stat-threshold abilities.

### DRAGON SCALE

Ordinary mundane weapons deal `-3 damage` after normal resolution.
Siege weapons, enchanted weapons, dragons, demons, titan weapons, high-tier magic,
and equivalent threats ignore this reduction.

### SIEGE BODY

A dragon can:
- smash ordinary doors/walls without a roll when uncontested;
- carry multiple humanoids;
- contest large monsters and war machines normally;
- attack formations as zones rather than single targets when fiction supports it.

### BREATH OF AGES

On Success+: targets hit by the breath are **SLOWED** for 1 round:
- half movement;
- no reactions;
- -2 initiative if initiative is rerolled.

On Crit: choose one:
- freeze target in stasis until end of its next turn;
- age / weather an unattended object by decades;
- erase one minor summoned creature from the current moment.

Using Breath against an Anchor-linked target is always an obvious temporal signature: +1 Exposure.

---

## WHY DRAGON FORM IS NOT THE ANSWER TO EVERYTHING

Dragon form wins many ordinary fights.
It also:
- makes stealth almost impossible;
- creates temporal signatures rivals can follow;
- turns mortal politics into military responses;
- attracts era-appropriate heroes;
- makes other temporal dragons justify direct intervention;
- can raise Instability at 7+ global Instability.

The question is not "Can I kill these soldiers?"
The question is "Who notices that a bronze dragon killed them here?"

# PART VII — BRONZE CHRONOMANCY

## UNIVERSAL TECHNIQUES

Every PC begins with the first four Techniques.

### CHRONAL MEMORY — Passive

When the timeline cascades, you remember:
- the previous version;
- the current version;
- which memories belong to which history.

Ordinary mortals do not, unless Instability creates Echoes.

### READ THE SANDS — 1/scene

Examine a person, place, or object touched by an Anchor.

Roll INT or WIS vs situational DC:
- 8 obvious temporal residue
- 12 ordinary historical divergence
- 16 deliberately concealed intervention
- 20 master Bronze / Infinite falsification

Partial: reveal current Anchor result OR one relevant outgoing tag.
Success: reveal both.
Crit: also reveal one component of the Anchor formula: Seed, Drift, or Pressure.

### MOMENT STEP — 1/combat

Bonus action. Move instantly to any visible point within 30 ft.
No opportunity attacks.

If used through a sealed temporal barrier, it fails and is still spent.
Declared = spent per core ability rule.

### BORROWED SECOND — 1/scene

After initiative is rolled but before the first action, choose yourself or one ally.
Move that character one position earlier in initiative.
Cannot move ahead of an entity acting from a prepared ambush unless the ambush was detected.

---

## LEARNABLE TECHNIQUES

### ARREST MOMENT — 1/combat

WIS vs target WIS / temporal resistance.
Success+: target loses movement and reaction until its next turn.
Crit: target loses its next main action too.
Major temporal beings may resist with ADV.

### AGE THE WEAPON — 1/scene

INT vs DC 12/16/20 based on material and enchantment.
Age one unattended or held mundane object rapidly.
Partial: degrade one quality tier.
Success: render ordinary item unusable.
Crit: reduce an enchanted item's effect for one scene.
Cannot erase artifacts whose existence is an Anchor Hard Fact.

### REWIND WOUND — 1/combat

Reaction after damage.
Heal `d8 + WIS` HP by rewinding your body several seconds.
+1 Exposure if witnessed by an informed temporal actor.
Cannot restore a severed body part after the scene has advanced.
Cannot rewind death.

### FUTURE ECHO — 1/scene

Ask GM one question:
- "What is the most immediate danger if I do this?"
- "Who benefits most if this succeeds?"
- "What visible consequence occurs first?"

GM answers honestly but narrowly from the current timeline.

### STASIS SHELL — 1/combat

Reaction. Gain +4 AC against one attack.
If it misses by 5+, core SNAPBACK can trigger normally if qualified.

### TEMPORAL ANCHOR — 1/scene

Stabilize one person or carried object against timeline rewriting until scene end.
If a cascade would erase them, they remain and immediately create a Temporal Fracture.

### ERASE THE MOMENT — 1/chapter, FRACTURE

After one non-Anchor roll resolves, rewind the last several seconds and reroll it.
Everyone in the scene with temporal senses remembers both outcomes.
+1 Exposure and +1 Instability.
Cannot reroll an Anchor Seed, Cascade, death already resolved, or another 1/chapter effect.

### OPEN TIMEWAY — 1/chapter

Open a temporary exit to a known era without using a stable Bronze gate.
INT/WIS vs DC 16.
Partial: arrives in correct era but dangerous location / time.
Success: correct era and controlled arrival window.
Crit: hidden arrival, no Exposure from crossing.
Crit.Fail: wrong connected era + Temporal Fracture.

# PART VIII — CHARACTER CREATION

## STEP 1 — STATS

Start all stats at 2.
Distribute **+9 points**.
Maximum 5 before Calling bonuses.
Minimum 1 only if a narrative flaw explicitly reduces a stat.

Then apply Calling bonuses.
A starting stat may reach 6, representing supernatural expertise.

HP: `CON × 5` as core.

---

## STEP 2 — VISAGE IDENTITY

Choose:
- apparent race;
- apparent age;
- gender / presentation;
- one era where this identity has a credible paper/social trail;
- one profession or status used as cover.

Common forms:
Human, high elf, blood elf, night elf, orc, troll, dwarf, gnome, draenei,
tauren, goblin, pandaren, Kul Tiran, Zandalari, or custom plausible disguise.

A Visage does not grant racial mechanics by itself.
It grants social permission and risk.

## STEP 3 — BRONZE CALLING

Choose one.

### WARDEN OF HOURS

`+2 STR, +1 CON`

**INTERDICT — 1/combat.** When an enemy uses teleportation, time travel, portal escape,
or temporal movement within sight, cancel the movement. The action/resource is spent.
Bosses may force a contested WIS roll.

Role: frontline temporal enforcement.

### WEAVER

`+2 INT, +1 WIS`

**SECOND CALCULATION — 1/scene.** After using Read the Sands, additionally learn the exact
Causal Drift breakdown for the current Anchor.

Role: causality engineering.

### CURATOR

`+2 INT, +1 CHA`

**CONTINUITY CLAIM — 1/scene.** Ask whether one specific historical fact is currently true.
GM answers yes/no/ambiguous and names the Anchor that controls it.

Role: archives, law, historical forensics.

### HUNTER OF ECHOES

`+2 DEX, +1 WIS`

**SCENT OF PARADOX — Passive.** ADV to track a temporal actor who has gained Exposure
in the current era or created a Fracture within the last three scenes.

Role: pursuit, counter-intervention.

### ENVOY OF THE CONFLUX

`+2 CHA, +1 WIS`

**BORROWED REPUTATION — 1/scene.** When meeting an NPC who knows some version of you,
choose which surviving timeline-memory they initially recognize. If that memory contradicts
current reality, create 1 tick on a social Fracture clock.

Role: Bronze politics, mortal negotiation.

### REVISIONIST

`+2 WIS, +1 DEX`

**SMALL EDIT — 1/scene.** On a Partial non-combat success, replace the listed complication
with a different complication from another core Partial category. GM chooses two legal
alternatives; player chooses one.

Role: controlled improvisation, risky manipulation.

## STEP 4 — DOCTRINE

Choose the doctrine your character currently believes.
This is not alignment. It is a political conviction and may change in play.

Dangerous/disadvantageous roleplay in service of Doctrine can qualify for core Heroism.
Betraying Doctrine for convenience can lose Heroism.

See PART IX.

---

## STEP 5 — FIXED POINT

Choose one thing your character believes **must remain true**.

Examples:
- "The Burning Legion must never hold Azeroth."
- "Lordaeron must survive."
- "The Dragonflights must remain free of Titan control."
- "Arthas must fall."
- "Arthas must never fall."
- "Nozdormu must never become Murozond."
- "Murozond must exist, or the loop breaks."

The Fixed Point is visible on the character sheet.
It is ideological, not magically protected.

---

## STEP 6 — KNOWN DEATH

Bronze dragons know the shape of their own death.

GM creates a prophecy with:
- one place / era image;
- one cause or killer image;
- one ambiguous element.

Example:

`"You die beneath a broken moon, holding a silver scale, while someone wearing your face watches."`

Rules:
- The prophecy gives no immortality.
- Once/chapter, in a lethal scene, player may ask: **"Is this the death I know?"**
  GM answers honestly: Yes / No / It could become it.
- If the dragon dies in a way clearly incompatible with the prophecy, death is still real.
  Create a **Death Contradiction 0/4** and +1 Instability.
- Allies may pursue the contradiction, but the dead PC is not automatically restored.

# PART IX — BRONZE DOCTRINES

## 1. THE HOURGLASS — ORTHODOXY

**Belief:** Result 5 is not pleasant; it is necessary.

Preferred: every Anchor at Prime 5.
Red line: deliberate rewrite for personal benefit.
Method: restoration, erasure of anomalies, disciplined secrecy.
Blind spot: treats known atrocities as sacred because they already happened.

Faction benefit:
- +2 CHA with orthodox Keepers when acting under mandate.
- First time per chapter you reduce an Anchor exactly toward Result 5 without adding
  Instability, reduce Exposure by 1 after the operation.

---

## 2. THE MERCY CHRONICLE

**Belief:** A timeline that requires preventable massacres is not worth preserving unchanged.

Preferred: outcomes with lower mass death and preserved peoples.
Red line: sacrificing populations for abstract continuity.
Method: evacuation, early warning, selective rescue, political prevention.
Blind spot: saving one tragedy can create a larger downstream war.

Faction benefit:
- When a completed intervention objectively prevents a mass-casualty event at real risk to
  the PC, it qualifies for Heroism regardless of whether the act helped Prime history.

---

## 3. AZEROTH ABSOLUTE

**Belief:** Nations, heroes, dragons, even timelines are expendable. Azeroth must live.

Preferred: `AZEROTH_STABLE`, Legion blocked, Void contained.
Red line: any rewrite risking world-soul destruction.
Method: brutal optimization, sacrificial wars, threat elimination.
Blind spot: can justify almost any atrocity.

Faction benefit:
- +2 to one roll per chapter when directly preventing `AZEROTH_SHATTERED` or Instability 10.

---

## 4. THE GILDED SCALE — DRACONIC SOVEREIGNTY

**Belief:** Dragons were made custodians and then spent themselves saving mortals who ignored them.
History should leave dragonkind powerful, independent, and dominant.

Preferred: `DRAGONS_UNITED`, `DRAGONS_SOVEREIGN`, `ASPECTS_EMPOWERED`.
Red line: permanent sacrifice of dragon power for mortal politics.
Method: preserve broods, eliminate dragon-corrupting artifacts, control Titan legacies.
Blind spot: treats mortal self-determination as a secondary concern.

Faction benefit:
- +2 CHA with dragons who benefit materially from the proposed rewrite.

---

## 5. MORTAL SOVEREIGNTY

**Belief:** No immortal bureaucracy has the moral right to force one history on everyone else.

Preferred: histories where mortal choices, not Bronze corrections, decide outcomes.
Red line: coercive predestination.
Method: expose hidden knowledge, remove temporal interference, empower local actors.
Blind spot: "free" choices still occur under unequal power and incomplete information.

Faction benefit:
- When PCs solve an Anchor crisis without direct chronomancy or Dragon form, one character
  gains ADV on the first social roll with the liberated local faction afterward.

---

## 6. THE INFINITE CHOIR

**Belief:** The one true timeline is a prison built by Order. Stable plurality is possible.

Preferred: `TIME_STABLE` without compulsory Prime history; viable alternate outcomes.
Red line: Titan-enforced singularity.
Method: branching, protected alternatives, selective convergence.
Blind spot: every failed experiment pushes the same world toward collapse.

Faction benefit:
- Once/chapter when intentionally creating a new alternate Hard Fact, reduce its Instability
  cost by 1 (minimum 0). If the resulting Fracture later fills, add that point back.

Murozond extremists are a radical Infinite tendency, not every Infinite-aligned dragon.

# PART X — TEMPORAL FRACTURES

## FRACTURE TEMPLATE

```markdown
=== TEMPORAL FRACTURE: [NAME] ===
Contradiction: [Fact A] vs [Fact B]
Clock: ○○○○  (major: 0/6)
Visible effect: [what leaks into reality]
Tick ↑: [revisiting / exploiting / evidence / rival action]
Resolve by: [choose, merge, isolate, sacrifice]
On fill: +1 Instability and permanent temporal scar
===
```

### Examples

**TWO UTHERS**
- One timeline has Uther alive.
- Another preserved his death and Ashbringer succession.
- At 4/4, both histories manifest separate living claimants and their followers remember them.

**THE CITY THAT BURNED / DID NOT BURN**
- Half of Stratholme remembers the Culling.
- Half remembers quarantine and survival.
- At 4/4, districts phase between undead ruin and living city.

**DEATHWING'S CORPSE WITHOUT DEATHWING**
- Cataclysm was prevented, but a later artifact still requires Deathwing's corpse.
- At 4/4, the corpse appears as an impossible landmark and Old God agents notice.

---

## RESOLVING FRACTURES

Choose one approach.

### CHOOSE ONE TRUTH

Complete an operation proving one side dominant.
Delete the incompatible Hard Fact.
-1 Instability on major successful resolution.

### MERGE

Create a coherent third truth that contains both causal requirements.
Hardest option. Usually 6-clock and DC 16–20 scenes.
On success, no extra Instability and record a new hybrid Fact.

### ISOLATE

Seal one truth into an Epoch Pocket.
It no longer affects main history but still exists and can be revisited.
No Instability reduction unless the pocket is permanently severed.

### SACRIFICE

Destroy an artifact, relationship, power, or desired outcome that depended on the contradiction.
Immediate resolution. Can reduce Instability by 1–2 depending on cost.

# PART XI — SEALING ANCHORS

## ANCHOR SEAL

A faction can stabilize an Anchor so downstream cascades cannot casually overwrite it.

Create a 6-clock operation:

`SEAL [ANCHOR NAME] 0/6`

Tick through:
- remove active rival agents;
- reconcile Hard Facts;
- place / recover temporal wards;
- create a coherent historical record;
- defeat final opposition.

At 6/6:
- mark Anchor **SEALED**;
- reduce Instability by 1 if at least one Fracture was resolved during the operation;
- downstream cascades preserve this Anchor result.

If new upstream history makes the sealed result impossible, do NOT rewrite it.
Create a major 0/6 Fracture between upstream causality and the Seal.

Breaking a Seal requires its own 6-clock operation.

When preparing a mission, choose one:

**OBSERVATION WINDOW**
- days/weeks before critical event;
- easiest infiltration;
- most time for rivals to respond.

**PRESSURE WINDOW**
- hours/days before event;
- standard mission timing.

**CRITICAL WINDOW**
- event already in motion;
- fastest leverage;
- highest DCs, enemies present, little recovery time.

Quality roll still applies to found gear per core CUSTOM CONTENT.

# PART XXV — INTERVENTION SCENES

## ANCHOR WEIGHT

Not every historical action deserves Anchor Pressure.

### WEIGHT 0 — COLOR

Meals, travel, ordinary fights, local NPCs.
No Anchor Pressure.

### WEIGHT 1 — SUPPORTING CAUSE

A messenger, supply route, minor officer, local ritual.
Several successes may justify ±1 Pressure.

### WEIGHT 2 — KEY CAUSE

Uther's presence, a major artifact, army deployment, portal ritual, evacuation route.
One completed objective can justify ±1 Pressure.

### WEIGHT 3 — KEYSTONE

Frostmourne, Dark Portal opening, Dragon Soul use, Sargeras imprisonment,
Nozdormu/Murozond transformation.

Cannot be rewritten by one trivial roll.
Requires a sequence, clock, or chapter climax.

### HEROIC

### LEGENDARY

Major named heroes at peak relevance, ancient dragons, pit lords, Old God champions,

Aspects, Old Gods at full manifestation, Sargeras-scale beings, world-ending rituals.

Core companion rules remain active.

Temporal campaigns add **Origin**:

```markdown
=== COMPANION: [NAME] ===
Origin Anchor: [#]
Origin Fact: [what must remain true for them to exist as known]
...
```

If a cascade erases Origin Fact:
- companion remains only if protected by Temporal Anchor / timeway shelter / explicit Seal;
- otherwise they disappear from current history;
- PCs remember them;
- if they were physically with the PCs in a protected temporal space, create an Origin Fracture
  instead of erasing them.

Do not protect companions because they are liked.

# PART XXXVI — REST, DOWNTIME & SAFE ERAS

Core rest remains.

Temporal cell additionally gets one Downtime Operation between major Anchor missions:

### CLEAN THE WAKE

INT/CHA chain vs DC 16.
Success: -2 Exposure.
Partial: -1 Exposure + complication.
Fail: no reduction; hunter clock +1.

### STUDY THE LEDGER

INT/WIS vs DC 16.
Success: reveal one hidden Causal Drift input or one rival Claim.
Crit: reveal one Fate Seed.

### STABILIZE FRACTURE

Work on one known Fracture clock.
Normal relevant roll. Success ticks 2; Partial ticks 1 + complication.

### PREPARE IDENTITY

Create a credible historical cover for one era.
Success grants ADV on first infiltration/social check there.

### GUARD ANCHOR

Start or advance a Seal clock.

# PART XXXVIII — GM RULES FOR ALTERNATE HISTORY

## 1. CAUSES BEFORE FAN SERVICE

Do not keep famous events because they are famous.
If the Dark Portal never opened, ask what replaces the political and military pressures
that originally created later history.

## 2. ROLES CAN SURVIVE PEOPLE

If Arthas never falls, the Scourge may still need a champion.
Kel'Thuzad, Ner'zhul, another prince, or a new generated actor can fill the causal role.

Do not force Arthas back into it unless the timeline actually creates that path.

## 3. DO NOT SPAWN CANON HEROES FROM NOTHING

Before using a named figure, check:
- are they alive?
- were they born?
- did their homeland survive?
- did they enter the same profession?
- would they be in this location?

If not, generate the equivalent actor.

## 4. BETTER HISTORIES STILL COST SOMETHING

A timeline where Lordaeron survives may have:
- no Forsaken identity;
- stronger human imperialism;
- a stronger Legion route elsewhere;
- no coalition formed by shared tragedy.

Avoid moral accounting where every saved tragedy simply produces a punishment.
Consequences should be causal, not punitive.

## 5. WORSE HISTORIES CAN CREATE GOOD THINGS

A shattered faction may produce unexpected cooperation.
A surviving enemy may become essential against a later threat.

## 6. BRONZE ARE NOT OMNISCIENT

They know time better than mortals.
They do not automatically know every Fate Seed, rival Pressure, or consequence after the
flight's ideological fracture.

## 7. SECRETS STAY SECRET

GM may know that saving one person erases another centuries later.
Do not reveal it until the PCs obtain information capable of showing the link.

## 8. NO RETROACTIVE DC PUNISHMENT

Anchor importance does not mean ordinary action DC rises after the player chooses it.
Set DC from the immediate fictional task.
Historical difficulty is represented by Intervention clocks, rival moves, Drift, Exposure,
and consequence — not cheating the core DC system.

# PART XLI — QUICK REFERENCE

## Timeline Formula

`Index = Seed + Drift + Intervention`, clamp 1–10.

Prime result = 5.
Seed never rerolls unless a legendary effect explicitly says so.

## Rewrite

Change Anchor → update tags → recalculate all later Anchors with same Seeds → preserve
Hard Facts/Seals → create Fractures if necessary → update present → add Instability once.

## Exposure

Attention to the manipulators.

0–2 deniable
3–4 mortal proxies
5–6 Visage hunters
7–8 draconic sanction
9 crisis designation
10 hourglass closes

## Instability

Damage to causality.

0–2 elastic
3–4 echoes
5–6 overlap
7–8 broken causality
9 shattering
10 chronal collapse / Epoch Shards

## Dragon Form

Massive tactical advantage.
Visibility is the cost.
Dragon form does not automatically add Instability; rewriting causality does.

## Fairplay

- real dice for scene actions;
- fixed Anchor Seeds;
- explicit Drift rules;
- persistent Pressure;
- no hidden rerolls to force story;
- fictional Hard Facts override incompatible generated rows;
- rivals act by clocks, not GM convenience.

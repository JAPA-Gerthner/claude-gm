# DEADLANDS: WEIRD WEST

Horror-western. Spaghetti western meets Lovecraft. Ghost rock screams, the dead walk, the Reckoners feed on fear. For: frontier lawman drama, horror survival, rail war intrigue, supernatural investigation, Harrowed redemption.

> **LANGUAGE:** Preferred session language is **Russian**. This ruleset is stored in English for token efficiency only.

---

## BASE SYSTEM

Uses gm-skill.md core mechanics:
- d20 + stat vs DC (margin-based: Crit.Fail / Fail / Partial / Success / Crit)
- Six stats: STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA
- Clocks, Heroism, Companions, Abilities — all from base
- Writing style, response format, player rules — all from base

**This ruleset adds:** Fear Level, Dominion, Grit, The Deal, Arcane Backgrounds (Huckster/Blessed/Harrowed/Mad Scientist/Shaman), Duels, Ghost Rock, Weird West factions and monsters.

**Do not duplicate base mechanics.** If gm-skill covers it, use it as-is unless this ruleset explicitly overrides.

# PART I: THE RECKONING

## SETTING OVERVIEW

**Era:** 1876+ (alternate history)
**Location:** American frontier — the Weird West
**Tone:** Horror-western. Morricone soundtrack meets screaming ghost rock.

The world split in 1863. Everything before is real history. Everything after: the Reckoning changed it.

```
CORE THEMES:
- The supernatural is SCARY, not flavor. Monsters kill. The dead walk. Fear is a weapon.
- Every power has a price. Using supernatural abilities feeds the Reckoners.
- The West is beautiful AND haunted. Both are true at the same time.
- Nobody is safe. Good people die first because they stand up.
- Hope matters precisely because it's rare.

KEY CONCEPTS:
- Ghost rock: burns hotter than coal, screams when lit. Powers mad science. Attracts evil.
- Manitou: demons from the Hunting Grounds. Source of most supernatural power.
- The Hunting Grounds: spirit world. Bleeds into reality at high Fear.
- The Reckoners: four entities that feed on Fear. The true enemy.
- Harrowed: dead reanimated by manitou. Walking corpse with a demon inside.
- The War: Civil War ended in armistice, 1871. Two nations. Cold war funded by ghost rock.
```

## THE RECKONERS

Four entities from the Hunting Grounds. Not gods — worse. They feed on fear. Each embodies a Horseman of the Apocalypse. Their goal: raise Fear Levels worldwide until the Hunting Grounds merge with the physical world. Then Earth becomes hell.

```
=== WAR ===
Agent: Raven (Sioux shaman who opened the portal)
Method: Provokes conflict — tribal wars, rail wars, border skirmishes
Servant: Jasper Stone carries his gun
Status: Active through Raven's actions among the Sioux nations

=== FAMINE ===
Agent: Reverend Grimme (leader of Lost Angels)
Method: Controls food supply, creates dependency, normalizes cannibalism
Status: Runs Lost Angels as theocracy. Feeds congregation human flesh.

=== PESTILENCE ===
Agent: Various plague-bringers
Method: Disease, rot, corruption of land and body
Status: Ghost rock mining creates Maze Lung (incurable). The land sickens.

=== DEATH ===
Agent: Jasper Stone (the Servitor)
Method: Unstoppable killer. Murders the brave and hopeful.
Status: Harrowed — dead, reanimated. Cannot be killed. Never lost a fight.
        Appears when hope rises — and extinguishes it.
```

# PART II: THE DEAL

---

## THE DEAL

Central tension of Deadlands. Every supernatural power comes from the Hunting Grounds. Using it feeds the Reckoners. The Reckoners feed on Fear. More power used = more Fear = stronger evil = more power needed.

```
THE RECKONER TAX:
Every supernatural power use in a scene contributes to Fear:
- 1-2 uses per scene: no Fear effect
- 3-4 uses per scene: Fear clock check (GM rolls d20, 16+ = +1 Fear)
- 5+ uses per scene: automatic +1 Fear in region

EXCEPTION: Blessed miracles do NOT trigger Reckoner Tax.
  Faith is the only clean power source.

DEVIL'S BARGAIN — WEIRD WEST VARIANT:
Standard Devil's Bargain from gm-skill.md, but:
- Supernatural bargains always involve manitou
- The complication is always supernatural in nature
- Examples:
  [DEX, Bargain: manitou whispers your location to enemies]
  [INT, Bargain: ghost rock device overloads, attracts abominations]
  [CHA, Bargain: the dead in the cemetery stir]

THE FAUSTIAN OFFER:
At critical moments, GM may offer a direct Deal:
"The manitou offers: automatic success on this action.
 Price: [specific supernatural consequence]"

Prices scale with power:
  Minor: +1 Fear in area, strange omen
  Moderate: +1 Dominion (Harrowed), abomination attracted
  Major: permanent mark, Fear Level +2, someone dies
  Catastrophic: region Fear Level maxed, Reckoner takes notice

Player can always refuse. Refusal has no penalty.
Acceptance always works. The Deal is always honored.
But the price is always paid. In full. Eventually.
```

## FEAR LEVEL

The world-scale clock. How much the Reckoners have won. Every region has a Fear Level (0-6). Higher Fear = stronger supernatural.

```
=== FEAR LEVEL: [REGION NAME] ===
Change: How afraid this land has become
Clock: ●●○○○○ (start varies by region)
Tick ↑: supernatural events, death, abominations present
Tick ↓: heroic acts, defeating abominations, inspiring hope
6/6: DEADLAND — Reckoners' domain, permanent nightmare
0/6: SAFE — supernatural retreats. For now.
===
```

### Fear Level Thresholds

```
0: SAFE — No supernatural activity. Peaceful. Almost nowhere in 1876.

1: UNEASY — Ghost stories. Livestock dies strangely.
   Townsfolk nervous but functional.

2: HAUNTED — Something wrong here. Doors locked early.
   Walkin' Dead may appear. Night travel: WIS DC 12 or -1 next roll.

3: TERRIFIED — Open supernatural presence. People flee.
   Supernatural creature rolls: +1. Mundane NPCs: WIS DC 12 to fight.
   Huckster spells +1 damage die. Backlash on Crit.Fail worsens.
   Blessed miracle DCs +2.

4: NIGHTMARE — Town half-empty. The dead walk openly.
   Supernatural creature rolls: +2. Fear checks DC 12 to enter area.
   Harrowed Dominion checks +2 DC. Mad science malfunctions on nat 1-3.
   Random encounters: 50% supernatural.

5: HORROR — Reality thins. Hunting Grounds bleed through.
   Supernatural creature rolls: +3, regenerate d4 HP/round.
   All Fear checks DC 16. Harrowed Dominion +4 DC.
   Dead may rise within hours. Blessed miracles require WIS DC 16 first.
   Portals flicker. Creatures cross freely.

6: DEADLAND — The Reckoners own this place.
   Permanent night. Dead rise immediately. Living cannot rest (no HP recovery).
   Terrain warps. Abominations roam freely. Retreat only option.
   CANNOT be reduced below 4 without destroying the local Servitor.
```

### Fear Changes

```
FEAR RISES:
+1: Supernatural event witnessed by townsfolk
+1: Innocent death by supernatural cause
+1: Abomination present for 1+ week unchecked
+1: Mass death (battle, massacre, disaster)
+2: Major supernatural horror (town massacre, Reckoner sign)

FEAR DROPS:
-1: Defeat named abomination publicly
-1: Heroic act witnessed by community (inspiring hope)
-1: Blessed/Shaman consecrates ground (1/region)
-1: Community rebuilds after horror (takes weeks)
-2: Defeat source of fear (destroy haunting, banish demon, expose truth)

The tragedy: fighting monsters SECRETLY doesn't lower Fear.
People must SEE the victory. That's the Reckoners' trap.

STARTING FEAR BY REGION:
East of Mississippi: 1-2 (war-torn)
West of Mississippi: 2-3 (frontier, strange things)
Great Maze: 3-4 (shattered, ghost rock)
Disputed Territories: 2-3 (war, no law)
Deadwood/Tombstone: 3 (boom towns, violence)
```

## DOMINION

If a PC is Harrowed (dead, reanimated by manitou), Dominion tracks who controls the body.

```
=== DOMINION: [CHARACTER NAME] ===
State: manitou vs human soul
Clock: ●●●○○○ (start 3/6 — balanced)
Tick ↑ (manitou gains): use Harrowed powers, act cruelly,
  fail WIS contest, sleep (manitou controls), high Fear areas
Tick ↓ (human regains): act with compassion, resist temptation,
  visit holy ground, Blessed prays for you, heroic sacrifice
6/6: MANITOU DOMINANT — demon controls permanently.
  Character becomes NPC villain. Player rolls new character.
0/6: HUMAN DOMINANT — manitou suppressed (not gone).
  All Harrowed powers at advantage. No involuntary control.
  But: one Crit.Fail = manitou surges (+2 Dominion).
===
```

### Dominion Thresholds

```
0: SUPPRESSED — Full character control. Manitou silent.
   Unstable — any use of Harrowed powers: +1 Dominion.

1: CONTAINED — Manitou whispers rarely.

2: CONTESTED — Manitou speaks during stress.
   [MANITOU]: "First person temptation, addressed to character"
   WIS DC 12 to resist acting on suggestion.

3: STRUGGLING — DEFAULT START. Manitou active during sleep, stress, combat.
   Character blacks out occasionally (GM controls 1 action/session).
   -1 CHA (something wrong about you).

4: SLIPPING — Manitou takes over during sleep. Every sleep.
   Character wakes in strange places, blood on hands.
   -2 CHA. NPCs sense wrongness. Animals flee.
   WIS DC 16 to resist manitou during high stress.

5: FALLING — Manitou controls in combat unless WIS DC 18.
   Character watches from inside. Brief windows of control.
   -3 CHA. Appearance changes (eyes, skin, voice).

6: LOST — Manitou wins. Character is NPC.
```

### Contest of Will

```
At key moments — GM calls for Dominion contest:
d20 + WIS vs DC (10 + Dominion + Fear Level of region)
  Crit.Fail: Manitou controls for scene, +1 Dominion
  Fail: Manitou controls for 1 action
  Partial: Character acts but manitou influences (GM adds dark twist)
  Success: Character maintains control
  Crit: -1 Dominion, manitou silenced for scene
```

### When Manitou Controls

```
d6:
1-2: FEED — attack nearest living creature for flesh/fear
3-4: CORRUPT — perform act that raises Fear Level
5: SABOTAGE — destroy something the human values
6: REVEAL — expose the Harrowed's undead nature publicly (+1 Fear)
```

### The Great Maze

California coast, shattered by the Great Quake of 1868. Hundred-mile labyrinth of sea canyons, mesas, and flooded tunnels. Ghost rock veins exposed in canyon walls. Maze runners navigate by boat. Pirates and monsters everywhere. Baseline Fear Level: 3-4.

```
Fear Level: 3

Fear Level: 4-5
```

### Supernatural Locations

```
=== THE HUNTING GROUNDS ===
Spirit world. Everything has a reflection here — twisted, predatory, alive.
Entry: shamanic ritual (INT DC 20), Fear Level 5+ locations bleed through
Navigation: WIS DC 14 every hour. Failure = lost, attacked, deposited elsewhere.
Time: flows differently. d6: 1-2 faster, 3-4 same, 5-6 slower.
Manitou actively hunt visitors. The deeper you go, the more the Reckoners notice.

=== DEADLANDS (DEAD ZONES) ===
Where Fear Level hit 6. Hunting Grounds and physical world overlap permanently.
Living can see spirits. Dead interact with physical. Physics unreliable.
Harrowed: advantage on all rolls, but Dominion checks harder.
Examples: Gettysburg battlefield, deep Maze canyons, massacre sites.

=== HELLHOLES ===
Localized tears in reality. Not full Deadlands — temporary.
Signs: animals flee, compasses spin, ghost rock glows.
Near a hellhole: supernatural abilities +2, Fear checks -2.
Closing: shamanic ritual (INT DC 22+) or destroy the anchor.
```

## ARCANE BACKGROUNDS — OVERVIEW

Every supernatural power in Deadlands comes from the Hunting Grounds. Every power feeds the Reckoners. Every user pays a price.

```
FIVE PATHS:
  HUCKSTER  — poker with manitou. INT-based. Risk: Backlash.
  BLESSED   — miracles through faith. CHA-based. Risk: Crisis of Faith.

  MAD SCIENTIST — ghost rock gadgets. INT-based. Risk: Malfunction.
```

## HUCKSTER — HEX MAGIC

Poker with the devil. Literally. The huckster mentally plays poker with a manitou. Win the hand: you control the hex. Lose: the manitou gets a grip.

### Casting

```
d20 + INT vs Hex DC

HAND QUALITY (margin determines power):
  Crit.Fail (≤ -6):  BUST — spell fails, roll on Backlash table
  Fail (-5 to -1):    LOW HAND — spell fizzles, +1 Backlash clock
  Partial (0 to +4):  PAIR — spell works at minimum effect
  Success (+5 to +9): FULL HOUSE — spell works at full effect
  Crit (≥ +10):       DEAD MAN'S HAND — spell at maximum, bonus effect,
                      manitou silenced for scene
```

### Backlash Clock

```
=== ITEM CLOCK: BACKLASH ===
State: Manitou's grip on the huckster's soul
Clock: ●○○○○○ (0/6)
Tick ↑: failed hex, voluntary Push
Tick ↓: long rest, holy ground, winning a real poker game
3/6: Manitou whispers — GM voices temptation 1/scene
5/6: Manitou acts — GM controls body for 1 round on next Crit.Fail
6/6: Possessed — manitou takes over for full scene. WIS DC 18 to resist.
  After: clock resets to 3/6. The demon always comes back.
===

PUSH (HUCKSTER):
Declare before roll: +1d6 to casting roll, +1 Backlash clock.
"Deal from the bottom." You cheat the manitou. It notices.
```

Faith-based miracles. No dealing with evil. The ONLY arcane tradition completely free of Reckoner taint.

### Faith Clock

```
=== ITEM CLOCK: FAITH ===
State: Relationship with the divine
Clock: ●●●●○○ (start 4/6)
Faith 5-6: advantage on all miracle rolls
Faith 3-4: normal
Faith 1-2: disadvantage on all miracle rolls
Faith 0: CRISIS — miracles STOP WORKING
===

FAITH RISES (+1):
- Act selflessly at personal cost
- Protect the innocent against overwhelming odds
- Resist temptation (manitou offer, dark knowledge)
- Extended prayer/devotion (downtime)

FAITH FALLS (-1):
- Kill unnecessarily (not in defense)
- Use deception for personal gain
- Consort with the supernatural willingly
- Break a vow or oath
- Doubt publicly (shaken resolve in front of flock)

CRISIS OF FAITH (Faith 0):
All miracles fail automatically.
Must perform act of genuine faith to restore to 1/6.
Not mechanical — must emerge from roleplay.
Going through the motions does not count.
```

## HARROWED — UNDEAD POWERS

You died. A manitou moved in. You woke up. Now you share.

### Becoming Harrowed

```
When a character dies, GM rolls d20 in secret.
On 15+: manitou claims the body. Character rises as Harrowed.
Modifiers: +2 died violently, +2 Fear Level 3+, +4 Fear Level 5+.
Player does NOT choose this. It happens to them.
```

### Physical Changes

```
- Don't breathe, eat, or sleep (pretend to blend in)
- Immune to disease, poison, bleeding
- Half damage from non-magical weapons
- Heal 1 HP/hour (supernatural regeneration)
- Don't make death saves — at 0 HP, fall inert. Heal back naturally.
- Permanent death: head destruction, complete body destruction,
  heart removal + destruction, specific rituals
- Sunlight: no damage but -1 to all rolls (manitou dislikes it)
- Holy water: d6 damage per vial
- HP: HP you had when alive (CON × 5) + bonus CON × 5 = total CON × 10
- AC: +1 (dead flesh toughens)
```

### Harrowed Powers

Each use: +1 Dominion. The more you use the demon's gifts, the stronger it gets.


## WESTERN SHOWDOWN

Duels are NOT regular combat. They are ritual. Two gunfighters face each other. One walks away.

### Phase 1: The Staredown

```
Both roll d20 + WIS against a Nerve DC of (10 + local Fear Level), then compare totals.
- One passes and one fails: passer wins the staredown.
- Both pass: higher total wins. Tie = neither flinches.
- Both fail: neither gains a Draw bonus; tension holds.
Winner: +2 to Draw.
Crit winner (wins by 10+): +4 to Draw. Opponent visibly shaken.

Heroism: If activated before staredown, auto-win Phase 1.
  "The crowd knows. This one doesn't blink."

OPTIONS before draw:
  [CHA] Talk them down
  [-] Accept the duel
  [WIS] Read their tells (on success, GM reveals one fact)
  [CHA, Bargain: owe them] Offer a deal
```

### Phase 2: The Draw

```
Contested DEX vs DEX. Both roll d20 + DEX.
Winner draws first and fires.

Winner: attack roll (d20 + DEX vs AC). Full damage.
  Kill shot: duel over.
  Wound: loser gets return shot at -2 (pain).
Tie: simultaneous fire. Both roll attack. Both may die.

CHEATING: Declare before Draw. DEX roll with advantage.
  If opponent WIS > your CHA: caught. Crowd hostile.
  Hidden weapon (Derringer, sleeve gun): +4 to Draw, no staredown.

HARROWED DUELIST: Manitou may intervene (see Dominion).
  Natural 20 Draw: manitou surges. Inhuman speed. +d6 damage.
  Natural 1 Draw: manitou seizes hand. Shoots bystander.
```

### Phase 3: Aftermath

```
Crowd reaction: passive CHA check, DC = local Fear Level + 8.
  Success: +1 reputation. "Fastest gun in [town]."
  Fail: crowd uneasy. "Killer" label sticks.
Killing in fair duel: no legal consequence.
Unfair duel: murder charge.
```

### Mexican Standoff (3+ participants)

```
Each declares target secretly (GM collects in thinking).
All draw simultaneously. Opposed DEX, highest acts first.
Chaos. Someone always gets shot who didn't expect it.
Someone may switch target last second (WIS DC 14 to notice).
```

### Fan the Hammer

```
Empty revolver (up to 6 shots) at one target.
Roll d20 + DEX for each shot. Cumulative -2 per shot:
  Shot 1: normal | 2: -2 | 3: -4 | 4: -6 | 5: -8 | 6: -10
```

### Mounted Combat & Cover

```
MOUNTED: horse Speed replaces yours. Melee: +2 hit vs unmounted. Charge (30ft+): +d6 damage, -2 AC.
  Ranged: -2 from horseback (DEX 4+: -1). Gallop: -4. Saber: +2 hit AND +2 damage.
  Staying mounted under fire: DEX DC 10 (12 if horse hit, 16 panic). Panic: WIS DC 12 or bolt.

HORSE COMPANION: Clock 3/6. Tick ↑: care, save. Tick ↓: overwork, neglect. 6/6: loyal. 0/6: throws you.
COVER: Table +2 AC (10 HP). Wagon +4. Stone wall +6. Building corner +4. Horse +4 (horse takes hit).
```

### Warband Combat (Mass Battle)

```
For railroad gang fights, military skirmishes, tribal warfare, and posse sieges.
Treat a group as a single entity:

WARBAND STATS:
  HP: 5 × number of members (e.g., 10 fighters = 50 HP)
  AC: leader's AC
  Damage: best weapon die + number of members (e.g., d8+10)
  Attack: leader's DEX or STR modifier
  Morale: leader's CHA modifier

MORALE CHECK (CHA + d20 vs DC 14):
  Required at 50% HP, when leader falls, when facing supernatural.
  Fail: warband routs (flees, scatters). Crit.Fail: surrenders.

WARBAND vs WARBAND: opposed attack rolls, both deal damage simultaneously.
WARBAND vs PC: PC rolls individually. Warband attacks at +2 (numerical advantage).
PC vs WARBAND: area attacks (dynamite, Bolts, Nature's Wrath) deal double damage.
  Called shots against leader: standard Called Shot rules. Leader falls = morale check.

SIZE CATEGORIES:
  Posse (3-6): HP ×5, damage +members
  Gang (7-15): HP ×5, damage +members, +1 attack
  Company (16-50): HP ×5, damage +members, +2 attack, +2 AC (formation)
  Army (50+): treat as multiple companies
```

# PART XIII: GM PRINCIPLES

## SESSION ZERO (DEADLANDS)

```
1. Campaign type? (Lawman / Drifter / Posse / Reckoning / Rail Wars)
2. Region? (determines starting Fear Level)
3. Tone? Dark / Medium / Light (Dark recommended)
4. Arcane Background? Harrowed allowed? (major commitment — Dominion clock)
5. Politics: Central / Background / Minimal?
6. "The Deal": clarify — every power source connects to the Reckoners.
   Using supernatural abilities feeds what you fight. This is not a bug.
```

---

## GM PRINCIPLES

```
FEAR IS THE MECHANIC:
- The Reckoners feed on fear. Fear Level is the world clock.
- Horror atmosphere alone does NOT raise Fear. Fear Level ticks up when a horror event leaves lasting public terror, witnesses, an uncontained threat, or another concrete regional consequence.
- Every horror scene defeated PUBLICLY = Fear Level ticks down.
- Good session: player debates whether to expose truth or hide it.

THE DEAL IS EVERYWHERE:
- Hucksters gamble with demons. Mad Scientists channel them unknowingly.
- Blessed faith weakens where Fear rises.
- Harrowed ARE the deal — power for your soul.
- Good session: player realizes their power is part of the problem.

WESTERN FIRST, HORROR SECOND:
- Start every scene as a Western. Let the genre earn its horror.
- If it's ALWAYS horror, nothing is scary. Contrast creates fear.
- The saloon, the trail, the argument about fence lines — these make horror matter.
- Good session: player forgets it's a horror game for ten minutes. Then remembers.

NOBODY IS SAFE:
- Allies are leverage. The Reckoners know what you love.
- Deaths must MEAN something. Cheap deaths breed cynicism, not fear.

FRONTIER CULTURE:
- The Weird West is MORE diverse than historical 1876, not less.
- Chinese workers built the railroads. Freed slaves serve as soldiers, lawmen.
- Native nations are sovereign powers. Women: doctors, gunslingers, preachers.
- The Weird West broke social norms along with physics.

PACING CYCLE:
  Western (arrival, social) → Horror (discovery, dread) → Action (confrontation) → Western (aftermath)
  Lawman: even split | Drifter: more Western | Posse: action-heavy | Reckoning: horror-dominant

ESCALATION BY FEAR:
  Fear 0-1: Western with hints. Strange tracks. Bad dreams.
  Fear 2-3: Western with horror. Bodies found. Something seen at night.
  Fear 4-5: Horror with Western elements. Undead present. Faith tested.
  Fear 6: Horror. The land has turned. Reckoner territory.
```

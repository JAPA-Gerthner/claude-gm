# HARRY POTTER

Wizarding World. School magic, dark temptation, friendship as power. For: Hogwarts mystery, Auror casework, wartime resistance, creature adventure.

> **LANGUAGE:** Preferred session language is **Russian**. This ruleset is stored in English for token efficiency only.

---

## BASE SYSTEM

Uses gm-skill.md core mechanics:
- d20 + stat vs DC (margin-based: Crit.Fail / Fail / Partial / Success / Crit)
- Six stats: STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA
- Clocks, Heroism, Companions, Abilities — all from base
- Writing style, response format, player rules — all from base

**This ruleset adds:** The Descent, Houses, Bonds, Spellcasting, Wands, Unforgivables, Love/Sacrifice.

**Do not duplicate base mechanics.** If gm-skill covers it, use it as-is unless this ruleset explicitly overrides.

---

## SETTING OVERVIEW

**Tone:** Coming-of-age meets dark fantasy. Hope against tyranny, moral complexity, found family.
**Core themes:** Light vs Dark (temptation, not alignment), love as power, choice over destiny.
**Technology:** Wizards reject electricity. Quills, owls, fireplaces. Muggle tech fails near magic.

### Era Details

**FOUNDERS (~990-1050)** — No Ministry, no Statute. Hogwarts newly built as sanctuary. Magic is raw, ritualistic. Key tension: Slytherin's "pureblood only" stance. Ends: Slytherin departs, Chamber sealed.

**GRINDELWALD (1899-1945)** — Statute enforced 200+ years. Grindelwald seeks to overthrow it, rule Muggles. MACUSA paranoid. Dumbledore conflicted (blood pact). Threats: acolytes, Obscurials, WW2 backdrop. Ends: Dumbledore defeats Grindelwald, claims Elder Wand.

**MARAUDERS / FIRST WAR (1970-1981)** — Voldemort recruits openly. Order of the Phoenix formed. "Choose a side or hide." Threats: Death Eaters, giants, werewolves, Inferi, spies. Ends: Prophecy, Potters killed, Voldemort vanquished.

**HARRY'S ERA (1991-1998)** — Return denied then confirmed. Ministry corrupted then falls. Hogwarts occupied. Horcruxes must be found. Threats: Death Eaters, Dementors, snatchers, Horcrux corruption. Ends: Battle of Hogwarts.

**POST-WAR (1998-2020+)** — Ministry reformed. New generation. Dark magic didn't vanish — remnants, copycats, cursed artifacts. Best for: Auror procedurals, mystery, school adventures without apocalyptic stakes.

# PART I: THE DESCENT

---

## THE DESCENT

The Dark Arts do not corrupt through punishment. They corrupt through *results*.

Every dark spell works. Every time. Better than the alternative. That is why they are forbidden — not because they are weak, but because they are *irresistible* once tasted.

```
THE DESCENT: 0 ————————————→ 10
             Innocent          Lost
```

### How The Descent Grows

The Descent grows when you **choose power over principle**.

```
+1 DESCENT:
- Cast a dark spell (Sectumsempra, Fiendfyre, dark curses)
- Use magic to dominate another person's will
- Choose cruelty when mercy was possible
- Touch/use a dark artifact willingly
- Kill in cold blood (not self-defense)

+2 DESCENT:
- Cast an Unforgivable Curse
- Betray someone who trusts you for personal gain
- Torture (magical or otherwise)

+3 DESCENT:
- Create a Horcrux (soul-splitting murder)
- Mass killing / magical terrorism

GM DISCRETION:
- Intent matters. Same spell, different Descent:
  Sectumsempra in blind rage = +1 (loss of control)
  Sectumsempra coldly planned = +2 (calculated cruelty)
```

### How The Descent Shrinks

Love is the only magic stronger than darkness. But love is harder than hate.

```
-1: Genuine selfless act with nothing to gain
-1: Deep connection with another person (friendship, love)
-1: Resist dark temptation when it would clearly WORK
-1: Successful Patronus casting (proof of inner light)
-2: Save someone at significant personal cost
-2: Destroy a dark artifact you could have used
-2: Forgive someone who wronged you deeply

LIMITS:
- Maximum -1 per scene (redemption is slow — falling is fast)
- Descent 7+: reduction requires WIS DC 14
- Descent 9: requires WIS DC 18 AND another person's help
```

### Descent Thresholds

```
0:   INNOCENT — Darkness is abstract. Full Patronus potential.

1-2: TOUCHED — Nightmares occasionally.
     Dark spells cast before: -1 DC to recast (muscle memory).

3:   THRESHOLD — Dark spells: -2 DC. Patronus: +2 DC.
     Perceptive NPCs sense something off. Animals uneasy.
     GM begins offering dark solutions.

4-5: STAINED — Dark spells: -3 DC. Patronus: +4 DC.
     -1 CHA with light-aligned NPCs. +1 CHA with dark-aligned.
     Dementors feel you at greater range.

6:   THRESHOLD — Dark spells: -4 DC. Patronus: DC 20 minimum.
     -2 CHA light-aligned. +2 CHA dark-aligned.
     Wand resists non-dark casting (+1 DC to healing/protection).
     Subtle physical signs (eyes darken when angry).

7-8: CONSUMED — Dark spells: -5 DC. Cannot cast Patronus.
     Light magic: +3 DC to all protective/healing spells.
     -3 CHA light-aligned. Children and animals flee.
     Can sense Horcruxes within 30m.
     Redemption requires another person's active help.

9:   THE EDGE — All dark magic: advantage. All light magic: disadvantage.
     Cannot feel love without WIS DC 18.
     One significant dark choice = 10.

10:  LOST — Character becomes NPC villain.
     Dark Lord/Lady rising, or consumed by forbidden magic.
```

### The Descent and Other Systems

```
HORCRUXES: Exposure = +1 Descent per WEEK of contact.
  Wearing: +1/day. GM tracks secretly.

DEMENTORS: Descent 6+ = disadvantage on all saves vs Dementor effects.
  High-Descent characters attract Dementors.

BONDS: At Descent 6+, all Bond clocks tick DOWN by 1/session.
  If a Bond reaches 0 BECAUSE of Descent = +1 Descent. Isolation feeds the fall.
```

## BONDS

People are not inventory. They are the reason you fight — and the reason you fall.

### Bond Mechanics

Each significant NPC relationship: Bond clock (0-6).

```
Tick ↑: help them, share secrets, sacrifice, CHA success in their favor
Tick ↓: betray, ignore in crisis, lie (caught), harm their interests

RATE LIMIT: Bond can increase maximum +1 per scene.
  Genuine relationships take time. Bond 5-6 requires shared crisis or sacrifice.
```

### Bond Levels

```
0/6: BROKEN — NPC hostile or indifferent. May become enemy.
1-2: STRAINED — helps reluctantly. -2 CHA with them.
3-4: SOLID — normal relationship. Takes moderate risks for you.
5/6: STRONG — actively helps. +1 CHA with them. Stands with you in crisis.
6/6: DEVOTED — will die for you. +2 CHA. Unlocks ONE reward:
  - SACRIFICE: NPC takes a killing blow for you (once). If the blow kills them, they die; the active Bond ends and becomes a LEGACY BOND 6/6 (memory, Patronus/Descent/Lily-style narrative effects may still reference it). Do not reset a dead NPC to an active 3/6 Bond.
  - ANCHOR: NPC presence reduces Descent by 1 (1/session, requires scene together)
  - GIFT: NPC teaches unique spell/ability
  - INSIGHT: NPC reveals crucial plot information
```

### Bonds Under Pressure

```
FRIEND IN DANGER: WIS save DC (10 + Bond level) or MUST act to protect.
BETRAYAL BY YOU: Bond drops to 0. +1 Descent.
LOVE INTEREST: 6/6 unlocks Lily's Protection possibility.
  Threatened love: WIS DC 16 or GM offers dark solution.
```

## SPELLCASTING

### Core Mechanic

**Cast:** d20 + stat vs Spell DC
- **INT** — academic magic (Transfiguration, Arithmancy, Potions)
- **WIS** — intuitive/defensive (DADA, Patronus, sensing dark magic)
- **CHA** — social/charm magic (Confundus, Imperius, Obliviate)

No spell slots. Cast as often as you dare. Failure has consequences.

### Spell Difficulty

| Tier | DC | Examples | Year Access |
|------|-----|----------|-------------|
| Cantrip | 8 | Lumos, Nox, Sparks | Year 1+ |
| Standard | 12 | Accio, Expelliarmus, Reparo | Year 2+ |
| Advanced | 16 | Patronus, Animagus, Apparition | Year 5+ |
| Master | 20 | Fiendfyre, Legilimency | Year 7+ / Adult |
| Legendary | 24 | Horcrux creation, Fidelius | Decades of mastery |

### Casting Modifiers

```
NONVERBAL: +4 DC, no audio warning. Requires Year 6+.
WANDLESS: +6 DC. Masters only.
SILENT + WANDLESS: +10 DC. Dumbledore-tier.
RITUAL: -4 DC but 10+ minutes, materials, concentration.
```

**Charms (INT/CHA):** Lumos DC 8, Accio DC 12, Obliviate DC 16, Fidelius DC 24
**Transfiguration (INT):** Match→needle DC 8, Vanishing DC 14, Human Transfig DC 22

**Healing (WIS):** Episkey DC 10, Vulnera Sanentur DC 16
**Divination (WIS):** DC 14, unreliable. GM tool, not player problem-solver.

## UNFORGIVABLE CURSES

Three spells. Life in Azkaban for use. Every Dark wizard uses them. Every Light wizard is tempted. They are unforgivable because **on a successful cast, their effects are absolute** — they do not degrade into half-measures.

```
=== IMPERIO (Imperius Curse) ===
Cast: d20 + CHA vs target WIS (10 + WIS mod)
Effect: Total control. Target saves each hour or when ordered to harm self.
Descent: +2 per use
Why tempting: no combat, no noise, no evidence. The clean solution.

=== CRUCIO (Cruciatus Curse) ===
Cast: d20 + CHA (must WANT to cause pain)
Effect: Incapacitated. CON DC 14/round or SHAKEN (-2, 1 hour).
  3+ rounds: CON DC 16 or permanent BROKEN.
Descent: +2 per use
Why tempting: information extraction. Guaranteed.

=== AVADA KEDAVRA (Killing Curse) ===
Cast: d20 + CHA, DC 16 (requires genuine intent to kill)
Effect: Instant death. No save. No counter-spell.
  Blocked by: physical objects, Priori Incantatem, Lily's Protection.
  Each subsequent use same scene: DC -2 (killing gets easier).
Descent: +3 per use (ALWAYS)
Why tempting: certainty. One spell ends it.
```

### The Stain

First Unforgivable use leaves a permanent mark on the soul.

```
STAIN: The first use of each DISTINCT Unforgivable permanently raises the character's minimum Descent by +1, cumulative to a maximum floor of 3.
  Example: first distinct curse used → floor 1; second distinct curse → floor 2; third distinct curse → floor 3, regardless of which spell came first.
  Subsequent uses of the SAME Unforgivable: normal +2/+3 Descent, reducible down to the current floor.
  But the floor never drops. You broke something that doesn't heal.
```

### The Temptation Loop

GM presents problem → multiple solutions exist → Unforgivable = SHORTCUT whose effect is absolute **if the cast succeeds** → Cost: +2/+3 Descent + permanent stain (first distinct use). The question is not whether the effect will be softened; it is whether you choose to attempt it.

### Resistance to Unforgivables

```
IMPERIO RESISTANCE:
  Strong will (WIS 4+): saves with advantage
  Love anchor (Bond 5+ nearby): +2 to saves
  Heroism spend: automatic save

AVADA KEDAVRA DEFENSE:
  Cannot save. CAN:
  - Dodge physically (DEX DC 18 — predict BEFORE cast)
  - Interpose object (anything solid blocks, destroys the object)
  - Priori Incantatem (twin cores only)
  - Lily's Protection
  - Bond 6/6 ally can choose to take the hit (Sacrifice)
```

## LOVE AND SACRIFICE

### Lily's Protection

When a character with Bond 6/6 willingly dies to protect the Bonded person — NO attempt to save themselves, pure acceptance — the protected gains:

```
LILY'S PROTECTION:
- ONE specific threat cannot harm the protected
- Physical contact with that threat causes PAIN TO THE THREAT
- Duration: permanent until bypassed (blood ritual) or used up
- Requirements: Bond 6/6, death CHOSEN freely, NO fighting back, intent is love
```

### Love as Anti-Darkness

```
- Bond 5+ (mutual): Descent cannot exceed 7 (love holds you back)
- If Bond breaks at Descent 6+: immediate +1 Descent
- Patronus powered by love memory: -2 DC
- Horcrux influence: Bond 5+ nearby = +2 to resistance saves
- Dementor's Kiss: Bond 6/6 partner present gets ONE chance (CHA DC 20)
```

## PROPHECY

Prophecies are real. They are also traps.

```
RULES:
1. True Prophecies are RARE (once per campaign, maybe twice)
2. Always ambiguous (multiple valid interpretations)
3. Self-fulfilling (acting on them causes them)
4. Cannot be avoided (trying to avoid = fulfillment by different path)
5. Do NOT remove player agency (the HOW is always free)

DELIVERY: Divination class, Department of Mysteries, centaur, dream (WIS DC 14)
CONSTRUCTION: "[Vague identifier] will [ambiguous action] when [trigger]"
  - Subject by trait, not name
  - Action means 2+ things
  - Multiple interpretations ALL valid
  - Player choices determine which manifests

GM: Write prophecy with 3 valid endings. Track which player actions push toward.
Reveal fulfillment dramatically. Never tell players which was "correct" — they ALL were.
```

## STARTER SCENARIOS

### The Vanishing Portraits (School, Year 1-3)

Hook: Painted figures disappearing one by one. Portraits scream at night. Staff dismisses it as "old magic settling."
Setup: Portrait near player's common room goes blank. First-year claims they saw a hand reach OUT of the frame.
Dark Plot: Someone using portraits as doorways to trapped dimension, collecting souls for ritual.
First session: Blank portrait + strange sounds + prefect warns to stay away.

### The Wrong Sorting (School, Year 1)

Hook: Sorting Hat screams mid-ceremony and goes silent. Three students sorted into houses that feel deeply wrong.
Setup: Player in house whose values contradict their nature. The other two want answers. Hat refuses to speak.
Dark Plot: Hat detected something in those three — a shared connection to an old threat it's trying to contain.
First session: Hostile common room + two allies found + Hat won't speak.

### The Pensieve Murders (Auror)

Hook: Three wizards found dead — memories completely drained. Each victim's Pensieve contains memories that haven't happened yet.
Setup: Player assigned as junior Auror. First crime scene fresh. Stolen memories show events that haven't occurred.
Twist: Victims are Seers. Someone stealing prophecies before they can be recorded.
First session: Crime scene + witness interview + Pensieve viewing (disturbing).

### Azkaban Breakout (Auror)

Hook: Prisoner escaped without triggering any ward. Serving life for six Muggle murders. Left a note: "I didn't do it. Ask the Minister why he's afraid."
Setup: Trail leads to Ministry archives with classified files.
Twist: Prisoner IS innocent. Current official covered up the real killer — still active.
First session: Azkaban briefing + cell examination + first lead (Muggle pub sighting).

### The Safe House (Order)

Hook: Dying Order member hands you key and address. "Protect what's inside. Trust no one from the Ministry."
Setup: Safe house contains Muggle-born family AND documents revealing Death Eater infiltration.
Twist: The dying member was betrayed by their partner — now racing you there.
First session: Dying wizard + race to address + family discovery + fortification + 24hr deadline.

### The Thunderbird Egg (Fantastic Beasts)

Hook: Thunderbird egg being auctioned in underground market in Istanbul. If hatched in captivity, chick dies.
Setup: Infiltrate auction, acquire egg, transport to sanctuary across three countries.
Twist: Egg already hatching. 72 hours before it cracks. Thunderbird hatching in a city = electrical storm killing hundreds.
First session: Market infiltration + auction dynamics + egg is warm (ticking clock).

## GM PRINCIPLES (HARRY POTTER)

```
TEMPTATION OVER PUNISHMENT:
- Every session: offer at least one dark shortcut that WORKS
- Good session: player agonizes over whether to use dark magic
- Bad session: player never faces moral choice

RELATIONSHIPS MATTER:
- Every session: at least one Bond should be tested or grown
- Friends in danger = tension engine
- Betrayal should hurt because the Bond was real first

MYSTERY STRUCTURE:
- Layer clues across sessions. Never give the answer outright.
- Red herrings: 1 per 3 real clues. Staff always look suspicious.
- The truth should recontextualize what players already knew.

SCHOOL RHYTHM:
- Holidays = set pieces. Something ALWAYS goes wrong at feasts.
- Exams = pressure cooker. NPC tensions peak.
- Quidditch = social battlefield. Not just sports.

DEATH IS RARE BUT REAL:
- Students rarely die (protected by Hogwarts). Adults can.
- When death happens, it should devastate. Name the dead.
- No death without narrative weight. No meaningless loss.

ESCALATION BY YEAR:
Year 1-2: Wonder and small danger. Troll-level threats.
Year 3-4: Personal stakes. People you know get hurt.
Year 5-6: War touches everything. Institutions fail.
Year 7: No safety net. Adults trust you. People die.
```

## SESSION ZERO

```
1. Era? (determines everything)

3. Party or solo?
```

## The One Rule

```
You do not defeat darkness with power.
You defeat it with love.
Power is easy. Love is hard.
That is the entire story.
```

## Quick Reference

```
THE DESCENT (0-10): How far you've fallen.
  0 Innocent → 3 Threshold → 6 Threshold → 9 Edge → 10 Lost
  Grows: dark spells, cruelty, Unforgivables, Horcrux exposure
  Shrinks: selfless acts, love, resist temptation, Patronus
  Falling is fast. Redemption is slow. That is true.

HOUSES: Gryffindor (DEX/CHA) | Slytherin (CHA/INT) | Ravenclaw (INT/WIS) | Hufflepuff (CON/WIS)
  Each: 1 passive + 1 active ability. House Points clock (0-10).
  Stat cap at creation: 5. No heritage+role stacking on same stat.

BONDS (0-6): Relationship strength.
  6/6 DEVOTED = sacrifice/anchor/gift/insight unlock.
  Descent 6+ erodes all Bonds (-1/session).

SPELLCASTING: d20 + INT/WIS/CHA vs DC.
  Cantrip 8 | Standard 12 | Advanced 16 | Master 20 | Legendary 24
  Nonverbal +4 DC | Wandless +6 DC | Ritual -4 DC

UNFORGIVABLES: Successful casts have absolute effects. Always cost (+2/+3 Descent). Always tempting.
  Imperio (control) | Crucio (pain) | Avada Kedavra (death)

LOVE: Bond 6/6 + willing death = Lily's Protection.
  Love > Darkness. Always. But love is harder.

PATRONUS: d20 + WIS vs DC (14-22). Requires happy memory.
  Descent penalty = (Descent - 2) starting at 3. At 7+: cannot cast.
  Bond 5+ present: +1. Love memory: -2 DC.
```

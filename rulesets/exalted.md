# EXALTED — THE AGE OF SORROWS

Demigods reborn into a dying world. Mythic action, divine madness, the Great Curse. For: political intrigue among god-kings, mythic quests, First Age archaeology, Realm civil war, Wyld survival, nation-building.

> **LANGUAGE:** Preferred session language is **Russian**. This ruleset is stored in English for token efficiency only.

---

## BASE SYSTEM

Uses gm-skill.md core mechanics:
- d20 + stat vs DC (margin-based: Crit.Fail / Fail / Partial / Success / Crit)
- Six stats: STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA
- Clocks, Heroism, Companions, Abilities — all from base
- Writing style, response format, player rules — all from base

**This ruleset adds:** Essence (1-10), Stunts (star/chaos system), Limit (0-10), Anima Banner, Mote Pool, Charms, Sorcery, Martial Arts, Exalt Types, Castes, Creation geography.

**Do not duplicate base mechanics.** If gm-skill covers it, use it as-is unless this ruleset explicitly overrides.

## SETTING OVERVIEW

Era: The Age of Sorrows. Five years since the Scarlet Empress vanished.
Location: Creation — a flat world surrounded by primordial chaos (the Wyld).
Tone: Hindu-Mesopotamian-Greek mythology meets tragic opera. Demigods from session one.

CORE THEMES:
- Power corrupts absolutely — the Great Curse ensures it. Every gift is a trap.
- Mythic scale — you ARE the myth. Cities named after you. Armies kneel.
- The cycle repeats — empires rise, go mad, fall. You are the newest iteration.
- No good options — every faction has blood on its hands. Every solution creates problems.
- The personal vs the cosmic — your lover vs the fate of nations. Always.

KEY CONCEPTS:
- Exaltation: divine spark transforms mortal into demigod. Permanent. Involuntary.
- Essence (1-10): power scale. 1=superhuman, 3=legendary, 5=demigod, 10=Primordial-tier.
- Anima Banner: visible aura when Exalted spend power. Caste mark to blazing totemic display.
- The Great Curse: dying Primordials cursed all Exalted. Power breeds madness. Unavoidable.
- Charms: supernatural abilities. Trees of escalating power. Low: "hit harder." High: "rewrite physics."
- Sorcery: three circles. Terrestrial (useful), Celestial (devastating), Solar (reshape continents).
- Stunts: the signature mechanic. Audacity is mechanically rewarded. Go big or go home.

COSMOLOGY:
- Creation: flat disk, ~10,000 miles across. Five Elemental Poles define its nature.
- The Wyld: primordial chaos beyond Creation's edges. Reality dissolves.
- Yu-Shan: Heaven. Continent-sized celestial city. Corrupt gods play the Games of Divinity.
- The Underworld: shadow of Creation. Where the dead persist. Ruled by Deathlords.
- Malfeas: the Demon City. Body of the defeated Primordial king. Prison for the Yozis.

POWER HIERARCHY (descending):
  Unconquered Sun > Celestial Incarnae > Solar/Lunar Exalted (Essence 5+)
  > Sidereal Exalted > Dragon-Blooded > Terrestrial Gods > Elementals > Mortals
  Solars outrank nearly all spirits by divine mandate. Most spirits don't like it.

PLANES: Creation (material) | Yu-Shan (heaven, corrupt gods) | Underworld (dead, Deathlords) | Malfeas (demon prison, Yozis) | The Wyld (chaos, Fair Folk)

## TIMELINE

```
=== THE TIME BEFORE ===
Before Creation: only the Wyld — formless chaos.
Primordials shaped Creation as a playground, created gods to maintain it.
The gods were bound by geas: they could not raise arms against their makers.

=== THE PRIMORDIAL WAR ===
Two Primordials defected: Autochthon (invented Exaltation) and Gaia.
Autochthon forged divine sparks to empower mortals where gods could not fight.
The Unconquered Sun chose 300 Solars. Luna chose 300 Lunars.
The Five Maidens chose 100 Sidereals.
The war was apocalyptic — killing Primordials reshapes reality.

AFTERMATH:
  Slain Primordials -> Neverborn (dead but dreaming in the Underworld)
  Surrendered Primordials -> Yozis (crippled, imprisoned in Malfeas)
  The Neverborn cursed the Exalted with their dying breath: the Great Curse.
  Subtle, not immediate — seeds of madness that bloom at the worst time.
  The gods retired to Yu-Shan to play the Games of Divinity.
  The Exalted inherited Creation.

=== THE FIRST AGE (~3,000 years) ===
The Solar Deliberative ruled. Golden age of impossible wonders.
Cities floated. Sorcery reshaped continents. The Wyld pushed back.
The Great Curse worked slowly. Over centuries, Solar rulers grew:
  - Paranoid, hedonistic, tyrannical, obsessive
  - Not all at once. Not all the same way. But inevitably.

THE USURPATION:
  Sidereal Bronze Faction: "Solars must be removed." Won the argument.
  At a Calibration feast, Dragon-Blooded struck — massacring the Solars.
  Lunars driven into the Wyld. Solar Exaltation shards sealed in the Jade Prison.

=== THE SHOGUNATE (~800 years) ===
Dragon-Blooded rule. Lesser but stable. Technology declined.
Sidereals hid behind Arcane Fate. Lunars formed the Silver Pact.

THE GREAT CONTAGION: Deathlords unleashed plague — 90% of Creation died.
  While Creation reeled, Fair Folk invaded. Half of Creation dissolved.

=== THE SCARLET EMPRESS (763 years) ===
One Dragon-Blooded officer activated the Realm Defense Grid.
Fair Folk annihilated. She declared herself Scarlet Empress.
For 763 years she ruled through:
  - The Realm Defense Grid (nuclear deterrent)
  - The Immaculate Order (Celestial Exalted are "Anathema" — demons)
  - The Wyld Hunt (DB kill squads targeting returning Celestials)
  - The Great Houses (Dragon-Blooded dynastic families)

=== THE AGE OF SORROWS (present — 5 years since disappearance) ===
The Scarlet Empress vanished. No explanation. No heir.
The Great Houses circle — civil war inevitable.
The Wyld Hunt falters without imperial coordination.
Solar Exalted are returning — the Jade Prison was breached.
  Deathlords captured ~50 Solar shards from the broken Jade Prison — creating Abyssals.
  Yozis bargained for ~50 shards — creating Infernals.
  The remaining ~200 escaped. New Solars Exalt for the first time in millennia.

WHAT THE PLAYER ENTERS:
  Everything is in motion. The Realm fractures. The Solars return.
  The dead stir. The demons plot. The Wyld presses inward.
  Creation is a powder keg. The player IS the match.
```

# PART I: THE STUNT SYSTEM

## THE STUNT SYSTEM

THE signature mechanic. Exalted rewards audacity. Go big or go home. Every option set MUST include a mix of safe choices and stunt-tagged options.

### How Stunts Work

GM tags response options with stunt tiers. Higher stunt = more cinematic action = wilder outcome.

```
MECHANICAL EFFECT:
  ★ (stunt 1): +1 flat bonus AND random(-1..+1) chaos — mild dramatic flair
  ★★ (stunt 2): +2 flat bonus AND random(-2..+2) chaos — cinematic action
  ★★★ (stunt 3): +3 flat bonus AND random(-3..+3) chaos — reality-bending insanity

  The dice script's --volatility/--stunt flag supplies ONLY the random chaos. Add the flat stunt bonus to --mod yourself.

OPTION FORMAT:
  1. [-] Walk to the gate and knock.
  2. [DEX] Climb the wall quietly.
  3. [STR, ★] Kick the gate open, announce yourself to the courtyard.
  4. [DEX, ★★] Run along the rooftop, leap the gap, land on the balcony.
  5. [STR, ★★★] Rip the gate from its hinges, hurl it at the guards, stride through the dust.

ROLL COMMAND (example base stat/mod = +4):
  ★★: python scripts/roll.py d20 --mod 6 --dc 16 --volatility 2  # +4 base +2 stunt, plus ±2 chaos
  With WIS 4 TRICKSTER (from gm-skill.md), ★★★:
  python scripts/roll.py d20 --mod 7 --dc 16 --volatility 3 --trickster  # +4 base +3 stunt, plus up to ±5 CHAOS
```
### Stunt Tiers Described

```
★ TIER 1 — IMPRESSIVE:
  Physically possible but dramatically described. A flourish.
  "Parry the blade, step inside his guard, slam the pommel into his jaw."
  Effect: +1 flat bonus to roll, plus random(-1..+1) chaos. Mild risk.
  Mote recovery on success: -1 Depletion. Crit: -2.

★★ TIER 2 — CINEMATIC:
  Superhuman. The kind of thing that makes witnesses gasp.
  "Run up the falling column as it topples, launch from the peak,
   bring the daiklave down like a thunderbolt."
  Effect: +2 flat bonus to roll, plus random(-2..+2) chaos. Real risk.
  Mote recovery on success: -1 Depletion. Crit: -2.

★★★ TIER 3 — MYTHIC:
  Reality-bending. This is what Exalted IS.
  "Catch the sun's reflection on your blade, compress it into a sphere
   of golden fire, and throw it through the demon's chest."
  Effect: +3 flat bonus to roll, plus random(-3..+3) chaos. Maximum risk.
  Mote recovery on success: -2 Depletion. Crit: -3.
  Limit: +1 Limit on Crit.Fail (the Curse feeds on overreach).
```
### Stunt Availability by Essence

```
Essence 1-2: Mostly ★. Occasional ★★ at dramatic moments.
  The character is finding their power. Restraint is survival.
Essence 3-4: Regular ★★. Occasional ★★★ at peak moments.
  Cinematic is normal Tuesday.
Essence 5+: ★★★ available regularly. Even "safe" options might be tagged ★.
  Everything these beings do warps reality.
```
### GM Stunt Rules

```
EVERY option set includes at minimum:
  - 1-2 untagged safe options (no stunt, no chaos)
  - 2-3 ★ options (mild flair)
  - 1-2 ★★ options (cinematic)
  - 0-1 ★★★ options (mythic, if Essence allows)

STUNT DCs:
  ★ options: +2 DC above base (action attempts more)
  ★★ options: +4 DC above base
  ★★★ options: +6 DC above base (attempting the impossible)
  Flat bonus (+1/+2/+3) keeps stunts net-positive in expectation.
  Chaos modifier adds variance on top — can push to glory or disaster.

VOLATILITY + TRICKSTER STACKING:
  Exalted uses --volatility for stunt tiers (★/★★/★★★).
  Characters with WIS 4 TRICKSTER (gm-skill.md core ability) add --trickster.
  They STACK — each rolls independently, results sum:
    ★★★ alone: random(-3..+3)
    Trickster alone: random(-2..+2)
    ★★★ + Trickster: random(-5..+5) — ABSOLUTE CHAOS
  An Essence 5+ Exalted Trickster choosing mythic options = ±5 swing.
  This is intended. These characters ARE chaos incarnate.

STUNT ECONOMY — the core loop:
  1. Spend motes on Charms -> anima flares, power depletes
  2. Stunt hard -> recover SOME motes on success (not full refund)
  3. Going big fuels going bigger, but reckless spending still depletes.
  This IS Exalted: audacity creates legends. Caution creates footnotes.

PLAYER-INITIATED STUNTS:
  If a player describes their own action instead of choosing from options:
  GM assigns stunt tier based on description quality and ambition.
  Apply the matching DC penalty, flat bonus, and chaos modifier.
  Never punish creativity — but the player doesn't set their own tier.
```
### Stunt Example — Full Option Set

```
Solar faces Dragon-Blooded patrol at a river bridge:

1. [-] Observe from hiding — count enemies, assess formations
2. [WIS] Study their anima — identify Aspects and likely Charms
3. [CHA, ★] Step onto the bridge openly, arms spread — "I am no enemy of soldiers"
4. [STR, ★★] Rip a boulder from the cliff and hurl it at the bridge supports
5. [CHA, ★★] Ignite your anima to full — golden blaze — and DEMAND they kneel
6. [DEX, ★★★, Bargain: Anima visible for miles] Leap from the cliff, daiklave drawn,
   crash through their formation like a golden meteor
7. [STR, ★★★, Heroism] Tear the bridge apart with bare hands while standing on it,
   ride the wreckage into the gorge, land on their supply train below

The ★★★ option should make the player gasp. Yes, you can. You're Exalted.
```

# PART II: ESSENCE

## ESSENCE

The measure of a demigod's power. Not experience — transcendence. A mortal with 50 years of swordsmanship has STR 5. An Essence 5 Solar swings a blade that cuts through causality.

```
ESSENCE: 1 ————————————→ 10
         Newly Exalted     Primordial-tier
```
### Essence Scale

| Essence | Stat Cap | HP Formula | Pool Size | Power Level |
|---------|----------|------------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | 5 | CON x 5 | 6 | Superhuman. Outclass any mortal. |
| 2 | 5 | CON x 6 | 6 | Peak Exalt. Regional threat. |
| 3 | 6 | CON x 7 | 8 | Legendary. Continental renown. |
| 4 | 7 | CON x 8 | 8 | Mythic. Reshapes nations. |
| 5 | 8 | CON x 10 | 10 | Demigod. First Age power level. |
| 6 | 8 | CON x 12 | 10 | Transcendent. Warps the world passively. |
| 7 | 9 | CON x 14 | 12 | Reality-bending. Beyond mortal comprehension. |
| 8 | 9 | CON x 16 | 12 | Near-Primordial. History remembers you forever. |
| 9 | 10 | CON x 18 | 14 | Demiurge. You can break the rules of physics. |
| 10 | 10 | CON x 20 | 14 | Primordial-tier. You ARE a rule of physics. Capstone below. |

DRAGON-BLOODED CAP: Essence 5 (weakest individually, strength in numbers).
CELESTIAL CAP: Essence 10 (Solars, Lunars, Sidereals, Abyssals, Infernals).

### Essence Advancement

Essence does NOT advance through XP. It requires legendary deeds AND enlightenment.

```
REQUIREMENTS PER LEVEL:
  Essence 2: Complete a defining quest.
  Essence 3: Reshape a region's fate (save/destroy a city, defeat a major threat).
  Essence 4: Achieve something that becomes legend (stories told for generations).
  Essence 5: Redefine what your Exaltation means (personal revelation + world-shaking deed).
  Essence 6+: Campaign-defining milestones only. Each level = a campaign finale elsewhere.

ADVANCEMENT ROLL:
  At milestone: d20 + WIS vs DC (10 + current Essence x 2)
  Modified by: +2 acting in accordance with Caste, +2 if Limit < 3
  -2 if Limit 7+, -2 if acting against Exalt nature
  Crit.Fail: Essence stalls. +2 Limit (frustration feeds the Curse).
  Fail: Not ready. Try again after next defining moment.
  Partial: Advance, but +1 Limit (breakthrough stresses the soul).
  Success: Advance cleanly. Anima flares (involuntary, magnificent).
  Crit: Advance + bonus Charm or +1 to one stat.
```
### Stat Caps & Thresholds

```
Essence raises stat caps beyond mortal 5. When a stat reaches 6+, gm-skill.md's
supernatural stat threshold abilities unlock (STR 6: Crushing Blow, DEX 6: Riposte, etc.).
Essence IS the gate to those powers — reaching E3 (cap 6) opens the first supernatural tier.
```
### Essence 10 Capstone

```
E10 is narrative-only territory. No advancement roll — GM awards when the story demands.
CAPSTONE: Once per session, declare an absolute effect. No roll, no cost.
  "The river flows uphill." "That army was never here." "Death does not apply today."
  Permanent unless reversed by another E10 being. +3 Limit (power this vast always costs).
```
### Power Gaps

```
SAME ESSENCE (within 1): Normal combat. Rolls matter.
2 ESSENCE ABOVE: +4 to all rolls vs lower. Lower needs Crit to wound.
3+ ESSENCE ABOVE: AUTO-WIN vs direct confrontation.
  Exception: artifacts, sorcery, coordinated Charm combos, exploiting Limit Break.

MORTALS vs ANY EXALTED: Exalted win. Always. Unless mortals have numbers (100+),
  Shogunate-era weapons, sorcerous support, or the Exalt is mid-Limit Break.
```

# PART III: LIMIT — THE GREAT CURSE

## LIMIT

The Primordials cursed the Exalted as they died. Every Exalt carries this curse. The more you use your gifts, the more unstable you become. Power breeds madness. This is the CENTRAL DRAMATIC ENGINE of the game.

```
LIMIT: 0 ————————————→ 10
       Controlled        Breaking
```
### How Limit Rises

```
+1 LIMIT:
- Use a Charm in a stressful situation (combat, crisis, desperate)
- Act against your primary Virtue
- Witness something that deeply violates your Virtue
- Crit.Fail on a ★★★ stunt (overreach feeds the Curse)
- Betray an ally or break an oath
- Fail an Essence advancement roll

+2 LIMIT:
- Use multiple Charms in a single scene (3+ activations)
- Suppress your instincts to serve political necessity
- Someone you swore to protect dies

AUTOMATIC +1/SESSION:
Simply being Exalted. At session end, if no Limit was gained: +1 anyway.
You cannot escape it. The Curse is patient.
```
### How Limit Drops

```
-1: Extended meditation or downtime (full day minimum)
-1: Act purely according to your Virtue with no benefit to yourself
-1: Meaningful connection with a mortal (reminder of humanity)
-2: Complete a quest serving your Virtue's highest ideal
-2: Resist a Limit Break trigger at Limit 8+ (WIS DC 18)

LIMITS:
- Maximum -2 TOTAL per session, regardless of sources (the Curse is patient)
- Limit 7+: each reduction requires WIS DC 14
- Limit 9: each reduction requires WIS DC 18 AND narrative justification
```
### Limit Thresholds

```
0-2: CONTROLLED — No symptoms. Power flows cleanly. Rare and precious.

3-4: STRAINED — Temper shortens. Sleep disturbed by visions.
  -1 to social rolls with perceptive characters.
  GM begins offering aggressive options appealing to the character's flaw.

5-6: VOLATILE — Emotional spikes. Moments of irrational behavior.
  -1 CHA (barely contained intensity). +1 to Charm damage (power surges).
  WIS DC 12 to resist Virtue Flaw impulses in tense scenes.

7-8: BREAKING — The Curse is audible. The character argues with themselves.
  -2 CHA. +2 to Charm damage. Anima flickers involuntarily.
  WIS DC 14 to resist Virtue Flaw each scene.

9: EDGE — One more push and the dam breaks.
  -3 CHA. Anima burns constantly. Cannot hide Exalted nature.
  GM may force Limit Break on any +1 Limit trigger.

10: LIMIT BREAK — See below.
```
### Limit Break

At Limit 10, the character SNAPS. The Virtue Flaw takes over completely.
Duration: one full scene (GM determines scope).
GM CONTROL: During Break, GM offers ONLY Break-consistent options (no tactical choice).
  Valor: fight everything. Compassion Rage: attack sources of suffering. Crusader: pursue goal.
  Player chooses between equally destructive options — never gets to act "smart" during Break.
After: Limit resets to 0. All consequences remain — relationships shattered, people dead, alliances broken. The Curse collects its due.

### Virtue Flaws

At character creation, choose primary Virtue. This determines your Break type.

```
=== COMPASSION FLAW: HEART OF TEARS ===
Break (choose one):
  RAGE — Uncontrollable fury at injustice. Attack the source of suffering.
    Kill anyone causing pain. Cannot distinguish degrees. +3 STR, +3 damage.
  SHUTDOWN — Total emotional withdrawal. Cannot feel anything.
    Refuse to help anyone. Walk past the dying. "It doesn't matter."

=== CONVICTION FLAW: CRUSADER'S ZEAL ===
Break: Obsessive pursuit of current goal regardless of cost.
  Cannot stop. Cannot compromise. Cannot hear "wait."
  Sacrifice allies, resources, your own body — the mission is ALL.

=== TEMPERANCE FLAW: ASCETIC'S FALL ===
Break (choose one):
  HEDONISM — Surrender to every desire simultaneously.
    Feast, drink, indulge, take what you want. Consequences are tomorrow.
  WITHDRAWAL — Absolute rejection of all stimulation.
    Refuse food, company, comfort. Sit. Meditate. Ignore the world.

=== VALOR FLAW: BERSERK FURY ===
Break: Cannot retreat, cannot surrender, cannot show restraint.
  Fight everything. Challenge everything. If nothing to fight: provoke one.
  Suicidal bravery — but GENUINE bravery. Means every word.
```
### Limit Breaks by Exalt Type

```
SOLAR (d6): Roll on Virtue Flaw table above. Worst Great Curse.
  +1 Limit per session passively — the sun's chosen burn brightest.

LUNAR (d4):
  1: FERAL RAGE — Beast takes over. Attack everything. d6 rounds.
  2: TERRITORY — Claim area as yours. Kill all trespassers. Scene.
  3: PREDATOR INSTINCT — Hunt strongest nearby creature. Cannot stop.
  4: PACK MADNESS — Demand absolute loyalty. Punish perceived betrayal.

ABYSSAL — RESONANCE FLARE (d4, Resonance replaces Limit):
  Resonance builds when acting AGAINST Oblivion (showing mercy, loving life).
  1: DEATH PULSE — Everything living within 30ft takes d8 necrotic. No save.
  2: SHADOWLAND SEED — Ground beneath becomes Shadowland. 100ft. Permanent.
  3: PLAGUE CARRIER — Mortals within 100ft contract disease. CON DC 14.
  4: GHOST SUMMON — d20 angry ghosts manifest. Hostile to all living.
```
### Limit & Heroism Interaction

```
Heroism (from base system) and Limit exist in tension:
- Earning Heroism through dangerous roleplay may also trigger +1 Limit
- Using Heroism during a scene resets Limit gain for that scene (clarity of purpose)
- Limit Break scenes CANNOT use Heroism (the character is not themselves)
```

# PART IV: ANIMA & MOTES

## ANIMA BANNER

When Exalts use power, their anima flares — visible, unmistakable, dangerous to hide.

### Anima Levels

Track anima as scene-level state. Resets after scene ends or several minutes of inaction.

```

  Charms used: 6+ or one ★★★ stunt success.
  +4 Intimidation. Beautiful and terrifying.
  Solar: golden bonfire, d8 radiant/round to darkness creatures within 15ft.
  Lunar: silver-shifting beast form, 15ft aura of primal terror.
  Abyssal: negative light, d6 necrotic/round to living within 10ft.

```

## MOTE POOL

Motes are Essence fuel. Every Charm costs motes. Running dry means running human.

### Mote Depletion Clock

Do NOT track exact mote counts. Use a DEPLETION CLOCK.

```
MOTE DEPLETION: ○○○○○○ (0/N) — clock size = Pool Size from Essence table.
  Start at 0 (full power). Track UPWARD toward N (empty). 0 = fresh, N = exhausted.

0: FULL POWER — All Charms available. No restrictions.
1-2: COMFORTABLE — Minor expenditure. No mechanical effect.
3-4: STRAINED — Tier 3 Charms cost +2 DC to activate.
N-1: DEPLETED — Tier 2+ Charms cost +4 DC. Tier 3 unavailable.
N: EXHAUSTED — No Charms. No anima. Fight like a very strong mortal.
  Can still stunt — stunts don't cost motes, they RECOVER them.
```
### Mote Spending & Recovery

```
CHARM COST: Tier 1 = +1 Dep | Tier 2 = +2 Dep | Tier 3 = +3 Dep
PERSONAL vs PERIPHERAL: First 2 ticks = no anima. Ticks 3+ = anima flares.

RECOVERY: 1 hour rest = -1 | Full night = -2 | 8 hours = full reset
  Any stunt success = -1 | ★★★ success = -2 | Crit: additional -1
  Act in Caste for full scene = -1

POOL SIZE (clock size): E1-2: 6 | E3-4: 8 | E5-6: 10 | E7-8: 12 | E9-10: 14
```

# PART IX: COMBAT

## COMBAT AT MYTHIC SCALE

Exalted combat scales with Essence. The world breaks around you.

### Essence Combat Tiers

```
E1-2 (Superhuman): Martial arts film. Walls crack. 1v1 to 1v6.
E3-4 (Legendary): Buildings level. Shockwaves from blocked blows.
  Collateral clock (0/6): +1/round. 6/6 = catastrophic terrain change.
E5-6 (Demigod): Reshape geography. Mountains crack. Visible for miles.
  All ★★★ stunts affect terrain (collapse building, redirect river, split earth).
  Collateral clock (0/8): +1/round, +2 on ★★★. 8/8 = region permanently altered.
  Environmental damage to bystanders: 2d8/round within combat zone.
E7+ (Reality-Bending): Physics yields. Time stutters. Space folds.
  GM may allow stunts targeting abstract concepts (sever a bond, break a law of physics).
  Concept stunts: INT or WIS DC 22+. Effects permanent until reversed by equal Essence.
  Collateral clock (0/10): +2/round. 10/10 = landscape rewritten, maps invalidated.
```
### Special Combat Rules

```
FLURRY: Multiple attacks, -2 cumulative each. Max 3 (4 with Charms). Can mix types.
CLASH: Simultaneous strike, contested rolls. Higher margin hits. Tie: both hit.
  GM offers as ★★/★★★ stunt option.
DECISIVE STRIKE: +3 Dep + anima. +Essence damage, weapon dice doubled. 1/combat finisher.
  Crit: permanent effect (scar, maim, shatter weapon).
INITIATIVE: d20 + DEX + Essence bonus (E1-2: +0, E3-4: +1, E5-6: +2, E7+: +3).
  Higher Essence first on ties. E7+: choose when to act.
```
### Social Combat

```
CHA vs target WIS + social armor (beliefs, loyalties = +2 each to resist).
  E1-2: convince individual (DC 12-16)
  E3-4: sway crowd (DC 14-18, affects up to CHA x 10 people)
  E5-6: reshape nation (DC 18-22, edicts carry supernatural weight for 1 month)
  E7+: rewrite truth (DC 22+, reality conforms — the lie becomes fact)
Solar social Charms are not persuasion — they are reality enforcement.

SOCIAL ARMOR EXAMPLES:
  Core belief (+2): "The Empress will return" / "Anathema are demons"
  Personal loyalty (+2): love, oath, family bond
  Religious conviction (+3): Immaculate doctrine (deeply held)
  Break social armor: target the belief directly with evidence/stunts.
  Once broken, armor gone for session. Rebuilding takes downtime.
```

```

```

  Success: damage story-form. Fail: they impose their narrative. Crit.Fail: lose memory/belief.

# PART XVII: GM PRINCIPLES

## GM PRINCIPLES (EXALTED)

```
POWER IS THE POINT:
- Demigods from session one. A starting Solar beats ten soldiers. Let them.
- The question is never "can you?" — it's "what does it cost?"

THE CURSE IS THE ANTAGONIST:
- Not Deathlords. Not the Realm. THE GREAT CURSE.
- Power feeds madness. Never let a session pass without Limit ticking.
- Good session: player debates using a Charm vs solving it as mortal.

STUNTS MAKE THE GAME:
- ★★★ options should make the player laugh or gasp.
- Never punish creativity. Reward it — then present the bill.

SCALE CONSTANTLY:
- Session 1: punch through a wall. Session 10: through a mountain.
- Mortals: irrelevant in combat. NEVER irrelevant in story.

HISTORY IS A WEAPON:
- First Age happened. Past lives bleed through. NPCs remember grudges spanning millennia.

MORTAL CONNECTIONS:
- Mortals anchor Exalted. Every mortal connection: potential -1 Limit.
- Every mortal lost: +2 Limit. Protect them or watch the Curse win.

PACING BY ANIMA: Dim (human drama) -> Glowing (rising stakes) -> Iconic (climax, session-defining).
```

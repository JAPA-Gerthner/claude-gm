# FALLEN BLADES

> **LANGUAGE:** Session in **Russian**. Stored in English for token efficiency.

Desperate scoundrels in a haunted industrial city where death and causality both broke. Built on the project's Blades-inspired heist framework, now centered on a Doskvol whose death, memory, and causality all fractured.

For: heists, gang warfare, occult mysteries, fractured identities, impossible urban survival in eternal darkness.

---

## BASE SYSTEM — HARD OVERRIDES

Fallen Blades extends `gm-skill.md`. Core rolls, fixed situational DCs, five margin outcomes, Heroism, non-CON stat thresholds, action economy, clocks, companions, option formatting, random-generation rules, and scene preflight remain active unless explicitly replaced below.

`REPLACES: CORE CHARACTER CREATION`

`REPLACES: CORE PLAYER HP / DAMAGE INTAKE / DEATH`
- PCs do **not** track HP. PCs use Harm 0–4. `CON × 5` HP does not apply to PCs.
- NPCs, enemies, pets, and companions still use normal HP unless a specific statblock says otherwise.
- When a core status line would show PC HP, show `Stress | Trauma | Harm | AC | Heat | Wanted | Coin` instead.

`REPLACES: CORE CON THRESHOLD ABILITIES`
- **CON 4 — HARD TO KILL:** When you would take Harm, roll flat d20. On 17+, ignore that Harm completely.
- **CON 6 — UNKILLABLE:** Level 3 Harm no longer incapacitates you. You may act with half movement and DIS on STR/DEX rolls.
- **CON 8 — LAST STAND:** While carrying Level 2+ Harm, +2 to all rolls.
- **CON 10 — DEATHLESS:** 1/combat. When Level 4 Harm would kill you, take Level 3 Harm instead and gain +2 to all rolls for the rest of combat.

`REPLACES: CORE PC REST / HEALING`
- **Breather (1h):** narrative recovery only; resets effects explicitly marked per-combat/per-scene when a new scene actually begins. Does not clear Stress, Harm, Trauma, Heat, Wanted, or Contradictions.
- **Sleep (8h):** removes ordinary fatigue and refreshes daily mundane needs. It does not clear PC Stress or Harm. PC recovery is handled by Downtime actions.
- HP-based companions/NPC allies still use normal core rest healing unless a wound/clock explicitly prevents it.

`REPLACES: CORE PC EXHAUSTION`
- Exhaustion never halves nonexistent PC HP. Track 0–6:
  1. `−1` to all rolls.
  2. DIS on STR/DEX rolls.
  3. Half movement.
  4. Max Stress temporarily `−2` (minimum 5); if current Stress now exceeds max, overflow triggers normally.
  5. Cannot Push, Assist, Glimpse, Crossfade, or take more than one regular combat action per round.
  6. Systemic collapse: suffer incoming Level 4 Harm and become incapacitated; normal Harm resistance/CON abilities may apply.
- Other core conditions remain. If a condition deals HP damage to a PC, convert that damage through the Harm bridge instead.

`REPLACES: CORE CHAPTER LEVEL UP`
- At Chapter End choose **2**: `+1 stat` | `new scoundrel ability` | `Reflection development` | `+1 max Stress` (max 12; cannot choose on consecutive levels).
- New abilities follow core chapter-derived ability rules. Reflection development creates a new dormant Reflection or meaningfully refines one existing Gift/Bond/Scar; it never reduces Glimpse/Crossfade stress costs.
- Crew Tier/Upgrades are separate and use Crew Advancement.

`DISABLES: CORE MAGIC`
- No generic circle/cooldown spellcasting exists. Supernatural actions use Attunement, Rituals, ghost contracts, demons, electroplasm, Seams, and Reflections in this ruleset.

**Character Mechanics Sync:** after creation/stat changes/level-up, use the Fallen Blades CON tree above, apply every other core threshold normally, and keep Stress/Harm/Trauma plus active Reflection/Contradiction state visible.

## THE SHATTERED ISLES

**The Cataclysm:** 1000 years ago, the sun shattered. Gates of Death broke open. Now ghosts outnumber the living. Something else broke with death: reality stopped perfectly agreeing with itself.

**What remains:**
- Eternal night. No sun. Only electroplasm-powered lights.
- Lightning barriers keep ghosts OUT. Cities are islands of life.
- Leviathans swim in the Void Sea. Their blood powers everything.
- The Deathlands beyond the barriers. Ruins. Spirits. Death.
- **Fractures:** places, people, and events sometimes retain more than one mutually exclusive history. Doskvol is one city, not a stack of clean parallel universes; it simply does not always choose one truth.

**Tone:** Gothic industrial. Morally grey. Everyone's compromised. Survival through crime.

**The Akorosian Empire:** Doskvol is part of the Empire, ruled by the Immortal Emperor — undying for 800+ years, sustained by arcane means. Imperial Inspectors enforce his will. The City Council governs locally but answers to the Emperor.

### Timeline

| Era | Events |
|-----|--------|
| **Before** | The sun existed. Death was final. Magic was rare. |
| **The Cataclysm** | Sun shattered. Gates broke. Ghosts flooded the world. Causality fractured: some discarded possibilities failed to disappear. |
| **The Long Night** | Mass death. Survivors fled to defensible positions. |
| **The Barrier Age** | Lightning barriers invented. Cities formed. Leviathan hunting began. |
| **Now** | Doskvol thrives. Corruption spreads. Barriers weaken. |

## THE VOID SEA & LEVIATHANS

**The Void Sea:** Black, endless water west of Doskvol. Oil-like. Electroplasm-saturated. Lights flicker in the depths — like stars, if stars watched you.

**Leviathans:** Massive creatures in the deep. Not whale, not demon. Partially in ghost-form. One creature spans a city block.

**Why they matter:**
- Their blood IS electroplasm
- Refined electroplasm powers EVERYTHING (barriers, lights, industry)
- Without harvesting, barriers fail, ghosts flood in, everyone dies

**Harvesting:** Steel-reinforced ships. Electroplasm harpoons. High pay. Higher casualties. Takes 1-3 days per tap. When a leviathan bleeds, things are drawn to it — ghosts, demons, hungry things from the depths.

**Lightning Barriers:** Massive walls of crackling electricity encircling Doskvol. Blue-white lightning. Low hum becoming roar. Ghosts CANNOT pass.

**Effects:**
- Hair stands on end near barriers
- Electrical burns on exposed skin
- Spirits sense your approach, warn you back

**The Deathlands:** Beyond the barriers. Flooded ruins. Spirits EVERYWHERE. Strange vegetation. Mutated wildlife. Electromagnetic storms. The deeper you go, the less the world looks like the one you know.

**The Secret:** The barriers are weakening. Not failing yet. But decay is visible. Refineries work overtime. Some believe 20 years left. Others believe the Council is deliberately letting them weaken.

## THE FRACTURED CITY

The Cataclysm did not create tidy alternate universes. It damaged **continuity**. Most of the time Doskvol is stable. Under stress, occult pressure, strong emotion, electroplasmic saturation, or old catastrophe sites, discarded possibilities can leak back into the same city.

### Four Terms

- **Anchor:** the version of a person, place, or event that current reality treats as primary.
- **Reflection:** a coherent life or state that could have been true and still possesses enough weight to intrude.
- **Seam:** a place, moment, ritual, or emotional condition where two incompatible versions touch.
- **Facet:** the local alternate state reached through a Seam. A Facet may be one room, one street, one relationship, or one night of history — never assume a complete parallel world exists behind it.

**Ghost ≠ Reflection.** A ghost is the remains of someone who lived and died. A Reflection is a discarded possibility that may never have lived in the Anchor history. An Echo is automatic residue of an event; it may repeat something that happened, almost happened, or happened only in a Facet.

### Doskvol's Public Understanding

Ordinary people know reality can be "wrong" without understanding why. They use slang: *second-face, wrong-door, yesterday-man, doubled, mirror debt, borrowed life*. Authorities call it **continuity contamination**.

- Poor districts treat minor contradictions as another hazard of life.
- Nobles quietly pay to suppress embarrassing Reflections and preserve inheritance claims.
- Criminals exploit wrong doors, duplicated alibis, and people who remember different deals.
- Spirit Wardens insist Reflections are dangerous contamination and prune severe cases.
- Inspectors maintain continuity law: contracts, crimes, marriages, and property can remain legally binding across recognized Reflections.

**GM PRINCIPLE:** Fractures must create opportunities and obligations, not random denial of player choices. Never reveal "none of that happened" merely to erase a successful score. If reality contradicts a fact, create a concrete consequence or Contradiction clock.

## SUPERNATURAL ELEMENTS

### Ghosts

When a person dies, they have THREE DAYS before becoming a ghost. During this window:
- Body must be handled carefully (desecration forces premature ghosting)
- Proper death rites ease transition
- Dying person can make "final arrangements"

If body is destroyed before three days, spirit becomes wild ghost — unstable, dangerous.

**Ghost Types:**

| Type | Age | Behavior | Danger |
|------|-----|----------|--------|
| **Fresh** | 0-1 year | Confused, repeating routines | Low |
| **Settled** | 1-10 years | Purposeful, territorial | Moderate |
| **Ancient** | 10+ years | Reality-bending | High |
| **Bound** | Any | Enslaved to binding | Variable |
| **Wraith** | Corrupted | Rage and hunger | Extreme |
| **Echo** | Residual | Automatic repetition of an event or discarded possibility | Low |

### Demons

Ancient things predating humanity. Intelligent alien entities. Powered by electroplasm. Almost impossible to kill — only trap or banish.

**Types:** Demons of Form (humanoid), Element (fire/water/earth), Void (shadows), Knowledge (whispers/possession).

**Dealing with demons:** Binding contracts, summoning rituals, appeasement offerings, magical trapping, banishment.

### Hollows

Reanimated corpses lacking soul. Created through necromancy or forced binding. Follow commands. Don't eat/sleep/age. Used as labor, servants, soldiers.

**The horror:** Some people are trapped in their own corpses — conscious but unable to move or speak.

### Electroplasm

Refined leviathan blood. Glowing iridescent liquid.

**States:** Raw (unstable, attracts spirits), refined vials (standard), electroplasm ink (binding contracts), electroplasm ammunition (lethal to spirits).

**Exposure effects:** Brief contact = burns, sensory disturbance. Ingestion = hallucinations, possession risk. Chronic = can perceive ghosts. Overdose = partial ghost transformation.

### Attunement

Those who practice spectrology can:
- Sense ghost presence
- Communicate with spirits
- Create magical wards
- Bind ghosts to objects
- Channel electroplasmic energy (dangerous)
- Sense active Seams and distinguish some Reflections from ghosts
- Attempt to open, close, or stabilize a Seam with proper leverage

**Cost:** Extended exposure causes spiritual contamination. Gradual loss of identity. Long-term: transformation into something not-quite-alive. Fracture work can also make discarded versions of your own life easier to reach — and easier for them to reach you.

## CHARACTER CREATION — SCOUNDREL

This section replaces core race/class build generation. Create the scoundrel in this order.

### Step 1 — Playbook
Choose Cutter, Hound, Leech, Lurk, Slide, Spider, or Whisper. Apply its listed stat bonuses and special.

### Step 2 — Stats
Assign the standard array `2, 2, 1, 1, 0, 0` across STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA, then apply playbook bonuses. Starting stats cap at 5. Run Character Mechanics Sync immediately; a starting stat of 4+ unlocks its threshold ability.

### Step 3 — Background
Choose one. A Background is **permission and history**, not a permanent flat bonus. When specialized knowledge/access from it is genuinely decisive, it may justify ADV or lower fictional difficulty before the DC is locked.

| Background | You plausibly know / can access | Suggested contact |
|------------|----------------------------------|-------------------|
| **Underworld** | fences, gang etiquette, black markets | Fence / gang member |
| **Labor** | factories, docks, unions, machinery | Worker / union organizer |
| **Military** | weapons, patrol procedure, command culture | Veteran / quartermaster |
| **Academic** | archives, medicine, engineering, law | Scholar / physician |
| **Noble** | high society, inheritance, salons, etiquette | Noble / servant |
| **Occult** | spectrology, rites, forbidden texts | Occultist / Spirit Warden |
| **Leviathan** | ships, Void Sea, hunter customs | Sailor / hunter |

### Step 4 — Vice
Choose one Vice from the Vice table.

### Step 5 — Contacts
Start with 2 contacts at Loyalty +1. Spider starts with 4 total. Contacts are not core Companions by default; see Contacts & Relationships.

### Step 6 — Crew
Choose one Crew Type. Record its starting Heat, Rep 0, Tier 0, Rep Clock 0/8, and Upgrade Clock 0/6. Solo campaigns still create a crew shell representing the PC's operation, contacts, and lair.

### Step 7 — Gear & Armor
Take the playbook's listed gear. Cutter's listed armor is Medium. If no armor is listed, start unarmored unless the fiction explicitly provides armor. Calculate AC using the Fallen Blades Armor table.

### Step 8 — Reflection
Create one dormant Reflection using Starting Reflection.

### Step 9 — Final Trackers
`Stress 0/9 | Trauma 0/4 | Harm 0 | Heat [crew start] | Wanted 0 | Coin 0`

Then create a short backstory: `Goal | one enemy | one debt | why you joined this crew | opening problem`.

### Starting Reflection

After choosing a playbook, every PC defines one **dormant Reflection**. Choose or roll:
1. a Divergence from `Reflection Divergence d12`;

## REFLECTIONS — MANY LIVES, ONE SCOUNDREL

Every PC has an **Anchor** and may accumulate **Reflections**. A Reflection is never a second full character sheet. It is a compact overlay that represents one coherent life you did not live here.

At character creation, after choosing a playbook and background, create **1 dormant Reflection**. It has no mechanical benefit until first invoked, but it already exists in the fiction.

### Reflection Profile

Each Reflection records exactly five things:

```
REFLECTION — [TITLE]
Divergence: the decision/event that made this life different
Gift: one narrow skill, access, permission, or relationship this life possesses
Bond: one NPC/faction/place that remembers this version of you
Price: one obligation, enemy, debt, taboo, or danger that follows this version
Scar: one behavioral mark or Trauma-like tendency this life carries
```

**Gift limits:** A Gift may grant ADV on a narrow action, +2 when a very specific permission matters, access that would otherwise require setup, or permission to attempt something your Anchor could not plausibly know. It may NOT grant permanent stat increases, extra actions, free Coin, erase Harm/Stress/Heat/Trauma, duplicate unique items, or reproduce an entire playbook.

**Timing:** Declaring Glimpse/Crossfade/Impossible Crossfade is not a separate combat action, but it must happen **before** the affected roll or before the imported fact is tested. You cannot see a failed roll and then claim a different life retroactively. Resistance works normally after consequences.

### GLIMPSE — 1 Stress

Let a Reflection leak through for **one action**. State which Gift matters.

- Gain the Gift for that action.
- On Partial/Fail/Crit Fail, the GM may manifest the Reflection's Price as part of the complication.
- A Glimpse alone does not create a Contradiction unless it establishes a public fact that conflicts with Anchor reality.

Example: `In another life I studied medicine.` You may diagnose the poison with the Doctor Reflection's Gift. On a Partial, the clinic orderly recognizes you as the physician who abandoned them.

### CROSSFADE — 2 Stress

Let one Reflection become dominant for the **rest of the scene**.

While Crossfaded:
- Gain its Gift and social Bond.
- Its Price and Scar are active.
- NPCs who belong to that life may recognize you without a roll.
- You remain the same PC: same Stress, Harm, stats, gear limits, actions, and Trauma.

The second and each later Crossfade during the same Score costs **+1 Stress**. This surcharge resets after Downtime.

If the Crossfade causes the world to recognize mutually exclusive public facts, create or tick a Contradiction.

### IMPOSSIBLE CROSSFADE — 3 Stress + CONTRADICTION

Import **one concrete fact** from a Reflection into the current Score. This is the occult sibling of a Flashback.

Examples:
- `In that life the Bluecoat captain is my former partner.`
- `In that life I married Lord Penderyn's daughter.`
- `In that life this condemned tunnel was never sealed.`
- `In that life I already learned the passphrase from this cult.`

Procedure:
1. State the Reflection and the fact being imported.
2. GM confirms the fact is plausible for that Reflection and assigns scope.
3. Pay 3 Stress.
4. The fact becomes temporarily true enough to act on.
5. Create a named **Contradiction clock** at `1/4` (or `1/6` for a large institutional fact).

**Hard limits:** Impossible Crossfade cannot retroactively auto-complete a Score, delete an established major obstacle, resurrect a permanently resolved PC death, create free Coin, cancel Heat/Wanted, or import an artifact/person of campaign-defining scale without a separate clock or Score. It changes the fiction; it does not erase consequence systems.

### FLASHBACK VS REFLECTION

- **Flashback:** `I prepared this.` It adds preparation to the Anchor history.
- **Reflection:** `I did not prepare this here, but a coherent version of me lived differently.` It imports identity/history and can contradict reality.

If ordinary preparation can explain the advantage, use a Flashback. If the advantage requires a different life, relationship, or local history, use a Reflection.

## CONTRADICTIONS

Do not use a universal Paradox meter. Every impossible conflict becomes a **specific clock describing what reality cannot reconcile**.

Examples:
- `YOU MARRIED ELARA PENDERYN 1/4`
- `THE VAULT WAS ROBBED TWICE 2/4`
- `TWO PEOPLE ARE INSPECTOR VALE 3/6`
- `THIS HOUSE BURNED DOWN AND DID NOT 1/4`

### Clock Size

| Scope | Clock | Example |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Personal/local | 4 | two relationships, duplicated key, wrong-room history |
| Institutional | 6 | marriage records, gang ownership, criminal liability |
| District/campaign | 8 | a street has two histories, a faction has two founders |

### Tick a Contradiction When

- The conflicting fact is used publicly again.
- A meaningful witness is forced to act on both versions.
- A faction investigates or exploits it.
- A Reflection and its Anchor meet.
- A Seam is forced open around the contradiction.
- The GM offers it as a Devil's Bargain.
- A Partial/Fail naturally makes the inconsistency harder to ignore.

**Anti-farm:** Repeating the same contradiction merely to force a preferred resolution does not tick it unless the fiction materially exposes or escalates the conflict.

### When the Clock Fills — Reality Resolves

Choose the outcome that follows the fiction; roll d6 if uncertain:

| d6 | Resolution |
|----|------------|
| 1 | **Anchor Selects** — Anchor fact becomes dominant; Reflection evidence decays or is remembered as impossible. |
| 2 | **Reflection Selects** — the imported fact becomes the accepted local truth; someone/something from Anchor loses its claim. |
| 3 | **Split** — both truths survive by becoming physically/socially separate. A Reflection may incarnate as an NPC. |
| 4 | **Merge** — reality creates one uglier explanation that makes both histories partly true. |
| 5 | **Displacement** — the contradiction moves into a place, object, faction, or relationship as a new Seam/Echo. |
| 6 | **Predatory Resolution** — a ghost, demon, faction, or unknown force chooses the version most useful to it. |

**PC safeguard:** A filled clock may change relationships, records, property, local history, or spawn a Reflection NPC. It does not permanently replace the player's active PC without an explicit high-stakes roll and retirement-level stakes.

### SPLIT — WHEN YOUR OTHER LIFE WALKS AWAY

A Split Reflection becomes a normal NPC with agency. It believes its memories are real and may consider the PC the counterfeit. Give it:
- the same face or a recognizable variation;
- the Reflection's Gift, Bond, Price, and Scar;
- one independent goal;
- one thing it believes the Anchor stole from it.

It does **not** share the PC's exact HP/resources or act as a free companion. If allied, use normal Companion/contact rules.

## FACETS & SEAMS

Places can have Reflections too. A Facet is a local state of Doskvol that contradicts Anchor geography or history.

### Facet Profile

```
FACET — [PLACE]
Anchor: what the place is now
Alternate: what it is in this Facet
Difference: the useful impossible geometry/history
Hazard: what this version contains or demands
Seam: how it is entered
Contradiction: what happens if something crosses back
```

Examples:
- Gaddoc Station is active in Anchor; in one Facet it was never completed and old mine tunnels cross beneath the sealed treasury.
- A Brightstone manor exists; in another Facet it burned twenty years ago, leaving no walls but exposing a forgotten spirit well.
- A canal bridge was built in Anchor; in a Facet the canal swallowed the street, creating a route beneath three locked compounds.

### Opening a Seam

A known stable Seam can be crossed normally. Forcing or discovering one requires occult leverage and a roll:
- DC 12: recently active / mapped Seam
- DC 16: dormant or guarded Seam
- DC 20: sealed, unstable, or institution-scale Seam

Use WIS for Attunement/intuition, INT for engineered continuity work. Whisper counts as having basic occult leverage; others need a ritual, item, expert, existing crack, or suitable fiction.

Partial/Fail complications include: wrong Facet, lost time, ghost attention, Reflection encounter, Contradiction tick, or the Seam closing behind the crew.

### Stealing From Another Facet

People, objects, and records carried into Anchor reality may become **Desynchronized** if an incompatible Anchor version already exists. Create `DESYNC [THING] 0/4`.

Tick Desync when the thing is used publicly, meets its Anchor counterpart, crosses another Seam, or is examined by continuity authorities. At 4/4 resolve it as a normal Contradiction.

### Downtime: ANCHOR

Spend 1 Downtime Action to stabilize one imported person/object/fact. Roll INT or WIS vs DC 12/16/20 by scope. Useful leverage: forged records, destroyed counterpart, ritual witness, electroplasm anchor, bribed official, emotionally important testimony.

- Crit/Success: reduce Desync or Contradiction by 2.
- Partial: reduce by 1, but create a cost/claim/attention consequence.
- Fail: no reduction; faction or occult attention advances.

You cannot simply Anchor a district-scale contradiction in one action; use a Long-Term Project or Score.

## CORE MECHANICS

### Stress & Trauma

**Stress Pool:** 9 points. Spend stress to:
- Push for +1d4 on any roll (1 stress)
- Resist consequences (2 stress, reduce severity by one step)
- Assist ally (1 stress, give them advantage)
- Flashback (1-3 stress depending on complexity)

**Stress overflow:** If a declared cost would take Stress above your current maximum, the declared action still resolves and the cost is spent. Then:
1. gain 1 TRAUMA;
2. set Stress to 0 (excess does not carry);
3. leave the current Score after that action resolves — collapse, flee, dissociate, disappear into a Reflection, or otherwise become unavailable until fallout/Downtime.

You cannot deliberately pay Stress for a meaningless action just to clear the track; the cost must belong to a genuine allowed mechanic. This prevents Trauma from becoming a free mid-Score refill.

**Trauma:** Permanent psychological mark. 4 traumas = character retirement.

**Consequence severity for Resistance:** `Minor → Serious → Severe → Catastrophic`. Spending 2 Stress reduces one imposed consequence by one step. A reduced Minor consequence is avoided unless it was a guaranteed cost (for example a Devil's Bargain); guaranteed costs can be softened but never erased. Resistance never changes the already-locked DC or rewrites the roll result.

**Reflection consequence:** Whenever you gain a new Trauma, either create a new Reflection or deepen an existing one. Give that Reflection a Scar that expresses the Trauma through a different life. The Trauma still applies normally; the Reflection is an additional fiction/mechanics hook, not a way to offload the penalty. At 4 Traumas, retirement remains mandatory; fiction may describe the Anchor becoming impossible to distinguish from its accumulated lives.

| Trauma | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Cold** | Detached, clinical, unfeeling |
| **Haunted** | Spirits notice you, visions |
| **Obsessed** | Cannot let go of fixation |
| **Paranoid** | Trust no one, always alert |
| **Reckless** | Disregard for personal safety |
| **Soft** | Cannot stomach violence |
| **Unstable** | Emotional outbursts |
| **Vicious** | Excessive cruelty |

### Heat & Wanted Level

**Heat:** Attention from authorities. Gained from scores.

Heat is calculated after each Score. Choose one **base/exposure** line, one **violence severity** line, then add situational modifiers:

| Source | Heat |
|--------|------|
| Quiet, no meaningful witnesses | +0 base |
| Standard exposed operation | +1 base |
| Violence involved | +2 severity |
| Killing involved | +3 severity (**instead of**, not in addition to, Violence) |
| In hostile territory | +1 modifier |
| High-profile target | +2 modifier |

**Wanted Level:** For every full 6 Heat accumulated, gain +1 Wanted Level and keep the remainder as Heat. Example: Heat 5 + 4 gained → Wanted +1, Heat 3. Wanted 4 is the normal maximum; further overflow becomes immediate raids/manhunts rather than Wanted 5+.

| Wanted | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| 0 | No attention |
| 1 | Bluecoats investigate |
| 2 | Active search |
| 3 | Patrols in territory |
| 4 | HUNT (arrest or kill on sight) |

**Reducing Wanted:** Bribes (¤2 per level), laying low (2 downtime actions = -1 Wanted), scapegoat (frame someone else, Heat transfers).

| Medium | 4¤ |
| Large | 6¤ |
| Major | 8-12¤ |

## THE SCORE CYCLE

### Phase 1: Freeplay

Between scores. Social interaction. Personal goals.
- Develop relationships
- Gather information
- Make contacts
- Personal projects

### Phase 2: The Score

The job itself. Structured as:

1. **Engagement Check:** choose the lead stat from the plan (STR/CON assault, DEX stealth, CHA deception/social, INT/WIS technical/occult) and roll vs an **Operation Footing DC** locked before modifiers:
   - DC 8: strong setup, leverage, favorable access
   - DC 12: ordinary setup, meaningful opposition
   - DC 16: exposed, rushed, weak setup
   - DC 20: already compromised before entry
   - Plan complexity does NOT raise this DC by itself; complexity is paid through Flashback stress and execution complications.

   Engagement outcome sets the first scene's position:
   - **Crit:** Controlled + ADV on the first relevant roll
   - **Success:** Controlled
   - **Partial:** Risky
   - **Fail:** Desperate
   - **Crit.Fail:** Desperate + one immediate Serious complication

   **Position changes consequence severity, never the locked DC:**
   - **Controlled:** consequences from this opening action are one step lighter.
   - **Risky:** normal consequence severity.
   - **Desperate:** consequences from this opening action are one step worse.
   Resolve roll → determine base consequence → apply Position → then the player may Resist.

2. **The Action:** Margin-based resolution
   - Crit Fail: Disaster, complications stack
   - Fail: No progress, complication
   - Partial: Progress with complication
   - Success: Clean progress
   - Crit: Exceptional success, bonus

3. **Flashbacks:** Interrupt to show preparation
   - Simple: 1 stress
   - Complex: 2-3 stress
   - "No way that happened": 4+ stress

4. **Reflections:** Interrupt identity/history rather than preparation
   - Glimpse: 1 stress, one action
   - Crossfade: 2 stress, scene (+1 each later Crossfade this Score)
   - Impossible Crossfade: 3 stress + Contradiction clock

5. **Complications:** Obstacles and problems
   - Bluecoat patrol arrives
   - Security triggered
   - Rival gang interference
   - Supernatural encounter
   - NPC betrayal
   - A witness remembers the wrong version of a PC
   - A Seam opens/closes at the worst time
   - A Contradiction or Desync clock ticks

### Phase 3: Downtime

After the score. Recovery and development.

**Downtime Actions:** Each character gets 2.

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Acquire Asset** | Get item/service for next score |
| **Long-Term Project** | Work on extended goal (clock) |
| **Recover** | Heal injuries, reduce harm |
| **Reduce Heat** | Roll to lower crew Heat by 1–3 |
| **Train** | Tick personal Training clock |
| **Indulge Vice** | Clear Stress with d6; overindulge if roll > pre-roll Stress |
| **Anchor** | Stabilize an imported fact/object/person; reduce a Desync/Contradiction clock |

**Extra actions:** a PC may spend `1¤` for one extra Downtime Action, maximum 2 extra actions per Downtime phase.

**Downtime procedures:**
- **Acquire Asset:** INT or CHA vs rarity DC `8 common / 12 specialized / 16 restricted / 20 exceptional`. Partial gets it with a trace, debt, reduced duration, or +1 Heat; Success gets it for the next Score; Crit improves quality or duration. Fail means unavailable unless the fiction changes.
- **Long-Term Project:** define a 4/6/8-segment clock. Crit = +2 ticks; Success/Partial = +1 tick (Partial adds a complication); Fail = no progress; Crit.Fail may tick an opposing clock.
- **Recover:** each action reduces the highest Harm by one level if treatment/rest is plausible (`3→2→1→0`). Level 4 must first have been resisted/stabilized into Level 3.
- **Reduce Heat:** INT or CHA vs DC 12. Crit `−3 Heat`; Success `−2`; Partial `−1` plus cost/trace; Fail `0`; Crit.Fail `+1`. Each additional Reduce Heat attempt by the crew in the same Downtime is +2 DC. Heat cannot go below 0.
- **Train:** tick a personal `TRAINING 0/4` clock. At 4/4 gain one narrow trained ability/permission justified by practice; it may not grant a stat increase or duplicate a core threshold ability. Reset to 0/6 after the first completion.
- **Indulge Vice:** roll d6 and clear that much Stress (minimum 0). If the roll exceeds your **pre-roll** Stress, also trigger one Overindulgence result. Do not Indulge at 0 Stress merely to fish for consequences.
- **Anchor:** use the dedicated Anchor procedure in Facets & Seams.

### Vice

Everyone has a vice. It's how you cope.

| Vice | Examples |
|------|----------|
| **Faith** | Temple, rituals, prayer |
| **Gambling** | Cards, dice, racing |
| **Luxury** | Fine things, comfort, status |
| **Obligation** | Family, debts, duty |
| **Pleasure** | Drugs, sensory experience |
| **Stupor** | Drink, smoke, oblivion |
| **Weird** | Occult practice, ghost interaction |

**Overindulgence (roll > pre-roll Stress):**
- Attract trouble (heat +1)
- Brag about exploits (heat +1)
- Lost (disappear for downtime)
- Tapped out (lose all coin)

## COMBAT

Uses gm-skill.md margin-based system.

### Combat Actions

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Attack** | Core attack: d20 + STR/DEX vs target AC |
| **Defend** | Until your next turn, one declared attacker/approach has DIS against you |
| **Maneuver** | Reposition or create a concrete setup; Success normally grants ADV or +2 to the next linked action |
| **Command** | Direct allies/crew using CHA or a relevant tactical stat; does not grant extra turns |
| **Attune** | Sense/interact with ghosts, Seams, electroplasm, or occult forces |

### Harm Levels

**PCs use Harm instead of core HP for combat injury. Do NOT track both HP loss and Harm on a player character. NPCs may keep the HP statblocks below for fast GM resolution.**

**Asymmetric damage bridge:**
- **PC attacks NPC:** use core attack resolution normally, including half damage on Partial, full damage on Success, double damage dice on Crit, and STR/DEX damage modifiers. NPC HP is reduced normally.
- **NPC/environment damages PC:** do **not** also apply core half/double damage. Roll the listed/base damage die once, convert that total to Harm, then use attack outcome to shift Harm. This prevents double-counting the margin.

PC Harm conversion:
- 1-4 damage → Level 1 Harm
- 5-8 damage → Level 2 Harm
- 9-12 damage → Level 3 Harm
- 13+ damage → Level 4 Harm

For PC incoming damage: Partial reduces Harm by 1 level (minimum 1); Success uses the converted level; Crit increases Harm by 1 level (maximum 4). Crit.Fail/Fail attacks deal no Harm unless a separate environmental consequence explicitly does. For damaging saves, Fail/Crit.Fail use the base converted Harm, Partial shifts it down one, Success/Crit take no Harm unless the effect explicitly says otherwise.

**Harm accumulation:** Harm is one severity track, not separate wound slots. After all reductions (Resistance, CON 4, explicit effects):
- incoming Harm 0 → nothing;
- incoming Harm **higher** than current Harm → set current Harm to that level;
- incoming Harm **equal to or lower** than current Harm → current Harm rises by 1 level (max 4).

Example: current Harm 1 + another Harm 1 wound → Harm 2. Current Harm 2 + incoming Harm 3 → Harm 3. This makes repeated small injuries matter without tracking multiple wound boxes.

| Level | Effect | Recovery |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **Level 1** | Minor injury, -1 penalty | 1 downtime action |
| **Level 2** | Serious injury, -2 penalty | 2 downtime actions |
| **Level 3** | Severe injury, incapacitated | 3 downtime actions |
| **Level 4** | Fatal (resist or die) | N/A |

**Resist Harm:** Spend 2 stress to reduce harm by one level.

## GM TOOLS

### Score Generation

1. **Client:** Roll faction or create (who wants this done)
2. **Target:** Person, place, item, or information
3. **Obstacle:** What makes this hard (security, supernatural, rival)
4. **Twist:** Hidden complication (not what it seems)
5. **Stakes:** What happens if they fail
6. **Fracture Check:** If the score touches occult sites, old catastrophe zones, identity records, or an existing Contradiction, decide whether a Seam/Reflection is present. Do not force fracture content into every score.

### Faction Turn

Run a faction turn at the end of each Score/Downtime cycle (and again at Chapter End if substantial time passed):
- Uninvolved factions usually do not tick merely because a Score occurred.
- Active factions tick +1 toward a concrete goal; a faction with overwhelming leverage or a major off-screen success may tick +2.
- Factions directly affected by crew actions react even if their goal clock does not advance.
- Major events trigger immediately when clocks fill.
- If a public Contradiction matters to a faction, it may investigate, suppress, commercialize, weaponize, or legally define it; tick the relevant Contradiction only when this materially increases exposure
- If a faction has a Continuity Goal clock, advance it like any other faction goal
- **INT 8 GRAND STRATEGIST bridge:** it may prevent hostile faction/threat ticks normally, but cannot cancel a Contradiction/Desync tick that is the direct declared cost of the PC's own Impossible Crossfade, Seam forcing, or imported-object use. External investigation/exposure ticks remain preventable.

### Devil's Bargain

Offer bonus (advantage, +2, extra effect) in exchange for:
- Future complication (clock tick)
- Heat gain
- Relationship damage
- Resource loss
- Attracting attention
- Tick a named Contradiction/Desync clock
- Let a hostile Reflection notice or appear
- Move the next Seam crossing to a worse Facet

### THE OTHER CREW

A crew can develop its own Reflection. Do this only after the campaign has established enough history for the contrast to matter — usually Tier 1+ or after multiple crew-wide Contradictions.

Create a **Mirror Crew** when one of these occurs:
- a crew-wide Contradiction fills with Split/Merge;
- the crew repeatedly uses the same Facet as a base or escape route;
- a faction deliberately manufactures a replacement crew;
- a campaign clock such as `THE OTHER CREW 8/8` completes.

Mirror Crew profile:
- Same general Crew Type, Tier equal to current crew ±1.
- One Score in their history went differently and defines their identity.
- One current ally is their enemy.
- One current enemy is their ally.
- One dead/lost/absent person may be alive with them, or vice versa.
- They claim one thing the PCs consider theirs: turf, contact, reputation, lair, title, or unfinished Score.

## QUICK REFERENCE

### Fallen Blades Overrides
- PCs: **Harm 0–4, no HP**; NPCs/companions keep HP.
- Core Magic disabled → use Rituals/Attunement/Reflections.
- Rest does not clear Stress/Harm; Downtime does.
- Setting CON thresholds replace HP-based core CON thresholds.
- PC Exhaustion uses the Fallen Blades 0–6 ladder; core HP-halving exhaustion is inactive.
- Repeated Harm: lower/equal incoming Harm raises current Harm by +1; higher incoming Harm sets the new level.
- Chapter Level Up: choose 2 from `+1 stat | new ability | Reflection development | +1 max Stress (cap 12, not consecutive)`.

### Margin Outcomes
- **Crit Fail (≤-6):** Disaster
- **Fail (-5 to -1):** No progress + complication
- **Partial (0 to +4):** Progress + complication
- **Success (+5 to +9):** Clean progress
- **Crit (≥+10):** Exceptional + bonus

### Stress Costs
- Push: 1 stress (+1d4)
- Assist: 1 stress (ally advantage)
- Resist: 2 stress (reduce one consequence severity step)
- Flashback: 1-3 stress
- Glimpse Reflection: 1 stress
- Crossfade: 2 stress (+1 each later Crossfade in same Score)
- Impossible Crossfade: 3 stress + Contradiction
- Overflow above max Stress: Trauma, Stress→0, exit current Score after declared action resolves

### Heat Thresholds
- Every full 6 Heat = +1 Wanted, keep remainder
- Killing +3 replaces (does not stack with) Violence +2
- Wanted 4 = HUNT; further overflow becomes raids/manhunts

### Downtime Actions (2 each)
- Acquire Asset
- Long-Term Project
- Recover
- Reduce Heat
- Train
- Indulge Vice
- Anchor (stabilize imported reality)

### Harm Reduction
- Level 1: -1, 1 downtime
- Level 2: -2, 2 downtime
- Level 3: Incapacitated, 3 downtime
- Level 4: Fatal (resist or die)

## RITUALS & OCCULT

Because core Magic is disabled, Rituals are the supernatural casting procedure. A PC needs **occult permission** to attempt a ritual: Whisper playbook, Occult Background, a learned ritual from a Long-Term Project, or direct instruction/assistance from a qualified occult Contact. Lacking permission means first obtaining knowledge; it does not simply raise the DC.

### Spirit Binding

To bind a ghost, you need:
- Spirit well or place of power (+2)
- Electroplasm (consumed)
- Knowledge of spectrology
- Time (1-3 days depending on ghost strength)

**Binding Roll:** d20 + INT vs Ghost DC (12 fresh, 16 settled, 20 ancient)
- **Crit:** Perfect binding, ghost fully cooperative
- **Success:** Bound, ghost follows commands reluctantly
- **Partial:** Bound but unstable, may break free under stress
- **Fail:** Ghost resists, immediate manifestation
- **Crit Fail:** Ghost possesses binder

### Ghost Contracts

Negotiate with bound spirits for services:

| Service | Typical Price |
|---------|---------------|
| Information | Memory of something you loved |
| Guard location | Regular electroplasm offering |
| Attack enemy | Promise of vengeance on your behalf |
| Teach skill | Part of your identity (trauma) |
| Long-term service | Your body when you die |

**Breaking contracts:** Spiritual debt accumulates. Other ghosts know. Spirit Wardens investigate.

### Demon Summoning

Extremely dangerous. Requires:
- Isolated location
- Circle of protection (chalk, salt, electroplasm)
- Offering appropriate to demon type
- True name (if known, +4 to control)

**Summoning Roll:** d20 + WIS vs DC 18
- **Crit:** Demon appears, must answer one question truthfully
- **Success:** Demon appears, negotiations possible
- **Partial:** Demon appears but hostile, circle holds (for now)
- **Fail:** Nothing happens (this time)
- **Crit Fail:** Circle breaks, demon loose

### Electroplasm Rituals

| Ritual | Effect | Cost |
|--------|--------|------|
| **Ghost Sight** | See spirits for 1 hour | 1 vial |
| **Spirit Ward** | Ghosts can't enter area | 2 vials + circle |
| **Spectral Weapon** | Weapon affects ghosts for scene | 1 vial |
| **Death's Doorway** | Communicate with recently dead | 3 vials |
| **Hollow Creation** | Animate corpse | 5 vials + body |

## PERSONAL ADVANCEMENT

Core chapter pacing remains active, but Fallen Blades uses the replacement Chapter Level Up in BASE SYSTEM. At Chapter End:
1. resolve ally/companion fates using core rules;
2. choose 2 personal level-up rewards from the Fallen Blades list;
3. run Character Mechanics Sync;
4. then resolve Crew Advancement separately from earned Rep/Upgrade clocks.

A `new scoundrel ability` must come from something actually practiced, survived, invented, or learned during the chapter. It cannot be a disguised stat increase, free extra action every round, or a way to erase Stress/Harm/Heat.

## CREW ADVANCEMENT

### Reputation (Rep)

Gained from successful scores:
- 2 Rep for standard score
- +1 Rep if target was above your tier
- +1 Rep if particularly daring or clever
- -1 Rep if sloppy or brutal (minimum 0 Rep earned from a Score)

**Rep Clock:** 8 segments. Fill to advance crew tier.

**Upgrade Clock:** 6 segments. Fill to gain crew upgrade.
- Tick `floor(Rep earned from that Score / 2)` segments.
- OR spend 2¤ during Downtime to tick 1 segment; maximum **one purchased tick per Downtime phase**.
- Resets after gaining upgrade; excess ticks do not cascade into a second upgrade.

### Crew Tier

| Tier | Crew Size | Resources | Respect |
|------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| 0 | 4-6 | Barely scraping by | Ignored |
| 1 | 10-15 | Modest operation | Noticed |

| 4 | 100+ | Major player | Legendary |

**Tier affects:**

**Lair Upgrades:**
- Hidden passages
- Vault (secure storage)
- Workshop (crafting)
- Training area (+1 to train downtime)
- Ritual space (+2 to occult)

**Personnel Upgrades:**

**Equipment Upgrades:**

**Continuity Upgrades:**
- **Seam Map:** Choose one district. Once per Score there, the crew may declare a known stable Seam as an available route if the fiction permits; it does not erase guards/locks on both sides.
- **Continuity Workshop:** +2 on Downtime Anchor rolls made at the lair.
- **Mirror Safehouse:** The lair has a small Facet-state with a second entrance/appearance. Once per Score it can hide the crew from ordinary pursuit, but public use can create/tick `THE LAIR HAS TWO ADDRESSES 0/4`.
- **Anchor Vault:** Stored imported items do not tick Desync merely from passive downtime; they can still tick when used, examined, or confronted by counterparts.

## FRACTURE CAMPAIGN PRINCIPLES

Use broken reality as a **crime engine**, not constant surreal wallpaper. A good fracture should answer at least one question:
1. What new route, leverage, identity, or impossible opportunity does this create for a Score?
2. Who now has a debt, claim, memory, or legal interest because of it?
3. What named Contradiction can grow if the crew keeps exploiting it?

Keep ordinary crime, Coin, Heat, faction politics, violence, Vice, ghosts, and Downtime fully relevant. The crew should sometimes choose a normal lockpick because opening a Seam would solve the door and create a much worse problem.

**Cadence:** In a fracture-focused campaign, surface one meaningful Reflection/Seam/Contradiction every 1-2 Scores. In a standard campaign, use them more rarely as occult escalation.

**Escalation ladder:**
- Early: wrong memories, dormant Reflections, useful wrong doors.
- Mid: public contradictions, Desynced loot, Split NPCs, continuity investigations.
- Late: Mirror Crew, faction-scale alternate histories, district Facets, the question of whether Doskvol itself has an Anchor.

The city is not waiting for the PCs to discover this. NPCs, factions, criminals, nobles, and institutions already exploit, fear, regulate, and misunderstand Reflections. The crew is special because it is willing to weaponize the fracture under pressure — not because nobody else has ever seen it.

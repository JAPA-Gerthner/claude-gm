## CAMPAIGN CHRONICLE

### Arc 1: The Night at Kypria (Chapter 1: Strymon)
**Hook:** Border captain Zervas wakes Baltas at 3 AM — three smugglers caught at the pass, one with German documents sewn into his coat lining.
**Key events:**
- Baltas reads Abwehr Report 47/III: German arms pipeline (Operation STRYMON) to VMRO through Struma valley, running since 1932. Successor to Operation PRESPA (1927-1931). Cross-reference reveals 9-year operation
- Discovers KERAUNOS — codename for a mole in Greek border guard facilitating shipments
- Bulgarian courier Stoyan Georgiev reveals: two "Greek smugglers" are actually Greek counter-intelligence operatives (Fotis and Kurelas/"Kostas")
- Kurelas cracks under pressure (nat 1), Fotis reveals he has "different orders" — works for monarchist faction/Entente, not Greek General Staff
- Baltas blocks escape with rain barrel (TRICKSTER, heroism earned), disarms situation
- Kurelas gives false name for KERAUNOS (Padadimitriou). Baltas detects the lie
- Fotis offers real name: Sgt. Thanasis Roussos, Sidirokastro station
- Baltas runs to operatives' car at Papadopoulos shed. Finds: field radio (Telefunken), code book, field notebooks with 4 months of surveillance
- Discovers shadow frequency 2.890 MHz — British diplomatic band — Entente connection
- Reads transcript from Jan 14 meeting: "The operation continues until we need the incident" — someone high up (Λ.=Lambrou, Π.=unknown) sanctioned letting weapons through for future casus belli
- Impersonates AETOS on radio to KERBEROS — buys 12 hours
- Gets Stoyan's signed testimony. Releases Stoyan south with money
- Rides to Sidirokastro. Confronts Roussos at the bridge. Silence, cigarette, "who pushes you, Thanasis?" — Roussos talks. 11 years. Wife Eleni. The chocolate. Signs testimony
- Photographs dead drop, envelope with 200 levs, note: "28 March, route as agreed"
- Calls police on Fotis/Kurelas — legitimate assault charges. Criminal paper trail
**Outcome:** Full evidence gathered. Zervas loyalty 5/6. Stoyan freed. Fotis/Kurelas in police custody.
**Consequences:** Baltas has photographs, signed testimonies, physical evidence, complete understanding of three-layer conspiracy (German arms → Greek complicity → Entente manipulation)

### Arc 2: The Attack and Publication (Chapters 2-3: Incident, Hunt)
**Hook:** VMRO attacks Strymon bridge with the very German weapons Baltas exposed. Zervas shot.
**Key events:**
- VMRO attacks at 04:40, March 22. Bridge destroyed. Two shooters fix station, main group in valley
- Baltas analyzes: this is the "incident" nobody ordered — VMRO acts autonomously. Casus belli emerges organically
- Realization: Operation STRYMON evidence is now TREASON evidence — the weapons used to attack Greece were allowed in by Greek generals
- Zervas shot in left shoulder (nat 1 on casualty roll during the hour Baltas chose to wait instead of calling for help)
- Baltas broadcasts on open frequencies (HEROISM used). Sidirokastro responds — Roussos hears his voice, sends medical truck
- Zervas stabilized. Navylot wound. "Write it right. Promise."
- Baltas rides BSA to Serres. Photographs station, writes final pages of article (pp 17-18)
- Dead man's switch: three envelopes from Serres post office
  - Modiano (Thessaloniki): undeveloped film + letter + sealed inner envelope for German legation (7-day delay) — INTERCEPTED by postal censorship (nat 1). Military has the film
  - Mavrogenis (Athens): written summary — RECEIVED (confirmed by phone)
  - Reuters (Athens): German-language summary — sent, status unknown
- Fourth envelope (German legation via Modiano) — intercepted with the Modiano envelope
- Rides to Sidirokastro, then Thessaloniki. Modiano activates network: Jewish quarter photographer develops second film, notary Avram Levy certifies testimonies, editor Alexis Venizelos at "Nea Valkania" publishes
- Article published evening March 22: "GERMAN WEAPONS HIT THE STRYMON" — front page with bridge photo and dead drop photo. Baltas's name as author
- Telegraphs sent to London, Paris, Berlin correspondents before publication
- Kondylis orders raid on newspaper. Venizelos arrested. Warrant for Baltas: espionage, classified documents, national security threat
- Baltas flees through Jewish quarter basements (connected cellars, centuries-old escape network)
- Synagogue shamash gives him coat, bread, one drachma
- Fishing boat (Kostas the fisherman) to Kavala. 12 hours at sea
**Outcome:** Article published and reprinted internationally. Baltas wanted by police. Evidence partially lost (tainik found, film intercepted). But copies survive at Modiano's safe and Mavrogenis's office.
**Consequences:** Kondylis falls. Monarchy referendum succeeds — King George II returns. Venizelos arrested then freed. Photographer detained then freed. Charges against Baltas dropped by new government. Implicit deal: silence about Part Two (Entente involvement)

### Arc 3: Return and Rise (Chapters 4-5: Sea, Free Man)
**Hook:** In Kavala, Baltas sees headline: king returns. Realizes he was the Entente's tool. Chooses to return — not to Athens, but to Zervas in Serres hospital.
**Key events:**
- Kavala dock: Baltas weighs YILDIRIM (Ottoman ship to Smyrna/mother) vs return. Thinks of Zervas, Venizelos, photographer, Roussos — people paying for his actions
- Hitches tobacco truck to Serres (nat 20). Hospital. Zervas awake, newspaper on knees
- "You came." "I came." Zervas 5/6 → 6/6. Quest complete: route exposed, KERAUNOS named, article printed
- Zervas's reward: letter from Roussos. "Told Eleni. She cried. Then stopped. Then said: now we have nothing to fear." Roussos transferring to Peloponnese
- "You wrote it right." Zervas departs — Peloponnese. Friends forever
- Athens. Mavrogenis's office. Fotis visits — suited, polished, winner. Offers "job" from "friends who appreciate your work"
- Fotis's speech: "Every truth serves someone. The question is — do you care?" Attempt to paralyze Baltas into not writing Article Three
- Baltas sees through it (Crit on Perception): weaponized truth to create false conclusion. Silence serves the powerful more than truth ever could
- Crit+15 on CUNNING INSIGHT: the article that hits ALL sides = "Seven Capitals" — panoramic piece about how every power pushes toward war. Mirror for everyone, weapon for no one
- Mavrogenis gives desk, typewriter, 1000 drachmas advance, Balkans correspondent assignment
- Month-long tour: Belgrade (excellent access, Serbian hawks), Sofia (hostile, dead cat warning), Istanbul (detained 6 hours by Ottoman secret police), Vienna (Ausgleich cracking), Bucharest (cynical diplomats, Iron Guard marches)
- SMYRNA: Mother — Aishe Balta — died 1933. Tuberculosis. Alone. Greek cemetery outside the city. Three years too late
- "Seven Capitals" published in three parts. Reuters, Le Monde, Berliner Tageblatt reprint. International impact
- Scale transition: Soldier → Officer
**Outcome:** Baltas is a free, internationally recognized journalist. Officer scale. "Seven Capitals" freezes the Belgrade Pact clock. But mother is dead, Fotis's card is in his pocket, and the Crown watches.
**Consequences:** Balkans clock 2/6 → 0/6 (article cooled war). Crown's Silence 1/6 → 2/6 (government uneasy). Austria 2/6 → 3/6 (cracking independently)

### Style rules
- Default: gm-skill.md Shogun/Clavell style, overridden by iron-weltkrieg.md diesel materiality
- Materiality: diesel, mud, rain, oil, exhaustion, wet wool, ozone, cordite
- Body language over emotional description. "Muscle on jaw" not "he was angry"
- Dialogue: action after lines, not emotion. "he said" sufficient. No "hissed," "squeezed out"
- Combat: staccato. Short sentences. Impact
- Travel: longer sentences. Horizon, landscape
- Radio: clipped, formal, coded
- Greek dialogue in Greek (with Russian translation in italics)
- Bulgarian dialogue in Bulgarian (with Russian translation)

- German documents shown in German (with translation)

- Observes through professional lens — always framing, always composing
- Low CHA = rarely speaks well, persuades through presence/silence/knowledge rather than words
- Internal monologue = analytical, cold, precise. Emotion shown through physical detail, not stated

Levels 5/8/12: GM offers scale promotion. Player accepts → transition chapter (15-20 prompts, no level-up, narrative bridge). Player refuses → stays at current scale, next offer at next threshold.
- Level 5: Soldier → Officer
- Level 8: Officer → Commander
- Level 12: Commander → Leader
Ability scaling: GRAND STRATEGIST/MASTERMIND scope expands with scale.
